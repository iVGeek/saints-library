Benedictine monk at the Saint Blaise of Hirsau monastery in the Black Forest. He led several brother monks to the new abbey of Engelberg nell'Unterwalden in Switzerland; he served there as its first prior and abbot. Spiritual teacher of the founder, Blessed Conrad of Sellenburen.
