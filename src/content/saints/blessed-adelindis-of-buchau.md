Married to Count Otto of Eesselburg, and mother of four sons. Widowed when her husband and three sons were killed in combat with invading Huns; their fourth son, a deacon, died soon after of natural causes. Adelindis founded Buchau Abbey in Württemberg, Germany, entered it as a nun, and later served as its abbess.
