Educated by the Salesians in his home town, when Augustin felt a call to religious life, he became a Salesian brother in Campello, Spain. He studied in Sarria, outside Barcellona, Spain, and made his profession in August 1923. He was assigned to houses in Valencia, Spain, and in addition to his teaching duties, wrote plays in his spare time. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
