Benedictine monk. First abbot of the Premonstratensian abbey in Steinfeld, Germany in 1184; he served the remaining five years of his life. Noted for his devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary, he wrote “Libellus de gaudiis Beatae Mariae Virginia”, a devotional to her.
