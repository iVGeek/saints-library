Augustinian friar. Sent to England in the mid-13th century to re-organize the houses there when Pope Alexander IV unified the various Augustinian congregations. That mission accomplished, he returned to Italy and became a noted preacher. Writings of the day describe a combination of personal piety and public zeal for the faith.
