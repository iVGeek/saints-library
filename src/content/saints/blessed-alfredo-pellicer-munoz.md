Franciscan Friar Minor, making his solemn profession on 5 July 1936, just days before the outbreak of the Spanish Civil War. He had been studying theolgy; his family urged him to give it up and become a teacher, in hopes of avoiding the persecutions of the anti–Catholic Republican forces. He refused and was soon arrested, told to deny God, and then killed when he refused to do so. Martyr.
