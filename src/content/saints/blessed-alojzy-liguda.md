A member of the Society of the Divine Word, joining in 1920 in hopes of missionary work in China or New Guinea; Brother Alojzy made his profession in 1926. Ordained a priest on 25 May 1927 and assigned as rector of the minor seminary of Górna Grupa in Poland. When the Germans invaded Poland in 1939, the seminary was converted into a prison camp for priests. On 5 February 1940, Father Alojzy and the other prisoners were transferred to the Dachau concentration camp in Bavaria, Germany where he spent his remaining days being abused for being Polish, Catholic and a priest. Martyr.
