Lord of Hauterives, Dr&ocirc;me, France. Father of Blessed Amadeus of Lausanne. With 16 of his men, he retired to become a Cistercian monk at Bonnevaux Abbey, and then he and his son moved to Cluny Abbey, both in France. Founded monasteries at L&eacute;oncel, Mazan, Montperout, and Tamis.
