Lifelong lay woman in the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain. Baptized on the day of her birth; confirmed in 1906 and made her first communion on 22 May 1907. Married to Army Captain Luis Maestre Vidal on 6 September 1924. Mother of three daughters. Member of Catholic Action. Widowed in 1927. Imprisoned, abused, starved and martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
