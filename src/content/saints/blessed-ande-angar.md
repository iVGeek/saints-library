Priest of the archdiocese of Paris, France. Earned a doctorate in theology in 1785. Vicar of the Saint-Sauveur parish. Arrested and murdered in the persecutions of the French Revolution for refusing to take the oath to the civil constitution. One of the Martyrs of September.
