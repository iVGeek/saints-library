Dominican friar, taking the habit in the church of Santísimo Rosario in Almagro, Ciudad Real, Spain on 3 October 1906, and making his profession on 9 October 1907. He studied at Almagro for the next ten years, and was ordained a priest on 21 September 1916. Missionary to Venezuela and Cuba. He was known for being dedicated to his ministries, and served as prior of his house. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
