As a young man, he studied with the Jesuits in his home town, then entered the Sulpician seminary of Laon in Paris, France. Priest in the archdiocese of Paris, France. Chaplain to the Ursuline house of Saint-Jacques in Paris. Arrested and executed in the persecutions of the French Revolution. One of the Martyrs of September.
