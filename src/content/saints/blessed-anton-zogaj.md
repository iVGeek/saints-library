Studied at the Pontifical French Seminary. Ordained on 26 April 1932 in the archdiocese of Tiranë-Durrës, Albania. Secretary of the Archbishop of Tiranë-Durrës. Vicar of Tiranë-Durrës. Arrested in Durres on 18 May 1947 in the Communist anti–Catholic persecutions. Martyr.
