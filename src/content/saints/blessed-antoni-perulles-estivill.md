Studied at the College of Saint Joseph in Tortosa, Spain. Priest, ordained in Burgos, Spain. Began work in the seminary in Orihuela, Spain in 1932; appointed its rector in 1933. Member of the Diocesan Laborer Priests of the Sacred Heart of Jesus. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
