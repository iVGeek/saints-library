Feeling a call to the religious life from an early age, Antonio began studying at the San Cecilio Seminary in the archdiocese of Granada, Spain from age 13. He felt a call to the Jesuits but would have been forced to move to Belgium to study; at the last minute he yielded to family pressure stayed in Spain. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
