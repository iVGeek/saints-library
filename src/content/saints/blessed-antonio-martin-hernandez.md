Raised in a pious family, Antonio felt called to the Salesian charism of teaching. After training in Salamanca, Spain and earning his certification as a teacher, he joined the Salesians. Ordained a priest in 1919. Antonio studied to be a Salesian and taught at Campello, Spain, and Carabanchel in Madrid, Spain. Novice master at the seminary in Sarria, outside Barcelona, Spain. Director of the Salesian college in Valencia, Spain. In all his assignments he was noted for his endless energy for the work and the mission. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
