Antonio joined the Dominicans in Almagro, Spain on 23 September 1917. He made his profession in September 1918, continued his studies, and was eventually ordained a priest. Beginning in 1926, he taught apologetics, moral theology, and served as librarian at the Almagro convent. He was a noted spiritual director to novices and students, continuing this support by extensive correspondence when these young people moved away from Almagro. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
