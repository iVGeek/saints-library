Aquilino studied at the seminaries of Coria and Toledo, Spain, and was ordained a priest on 25 August 1935. A member of the Hermandad de Sacerdotes Operarios Diocesanos (Diocesan Laborer Priests of the Sacred Heart of Jesus), joining in 1934 while still in seminary. He served as prefect of students in Baeza, Spain. On 20 July 1936 he was seized by anti–Catholic forces in the Spanish Civil War. Held a prisoner for several weeks, he was then murdered for the crime of being a priest. Martyr.
