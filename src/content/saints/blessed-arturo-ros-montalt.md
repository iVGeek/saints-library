Lifelong layman in the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain. Married to Maria Llopis Sirer at the church of San Honorato in Vinalessa, Spain on 26 November 1927. Father of six; one daughter became a nun, one son a priest. Leader of the Catholic Union. Founded the local chapter of Catholic Action. Imprisoned, tortured and martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
