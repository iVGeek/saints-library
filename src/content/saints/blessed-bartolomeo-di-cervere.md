After studying in Savigliano and Turin, Italy, Bartolomeo joined the Dominicans at Savigliano. He was ordained a priest in 1445. He earned three degrees in theology from the University of Turin, awarded all of them on 8 May 1452, and then taught at the university for a year. Chosen prior of the convent in Savigliano in 1453. He was known as a zealous and persuasive preacher, bringing many back to orthodox Christianity. Appointed Inquisitor in the Piedmont region of Italy; as violent as the area heretics were, this was effectively a death sentence. He was murdered by a gang of five Waldensians on his first day of in the region. Martyr.
