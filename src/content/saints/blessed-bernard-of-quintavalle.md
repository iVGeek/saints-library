Born to the wealthy nobility of Assisi, Bernard studied at the University of Bologna and received degrees in both civil and canon law, and fought in the First Crusade. He was one of the early followers of Saint Francis of Assisi, meeting him and becoming his spiritual student in 1208, after which he gave away all his wealth to widows, orphans, monasteries, pilgrims and hospitals. He was one of the friars who travelled with Francis to Rome, Italy to obtain approval of the Franciscan Rule by Pope Innocent III on 16 April 1209. He worked to bring Franciscan spirituality to Florence, Italy, and then to Bologna, Italy in 1211 where he worked to bring the message to the wealthy. He travelled with Saint Clare of Assisi as she tried to avoid being kidnapped and 'deprogrammed' by her family, and with Brother Giles to Spain to spread the Order there. Served as Franciscan provincial for Spain from 1217 to 1219. Known to have preached in Siena, Italy from 1241 to 1243.
