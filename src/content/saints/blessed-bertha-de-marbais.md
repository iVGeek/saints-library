Born to the nobility, and married to the prince of Molenbaix (part of modern Belgium. Widowed, Bertha felt a call to religion and became a Cistercian nun at Aywières Abbey, in the diocese of Namur, Belgium. Friend of Saint Lutgardis of Aywières. First abbess of the abbey of Marchet near Lille, Belgium in 1226, she served the remaining 21 years of her life.
