Though he was initially considered a pious and devoted youth, Bonaventure gave himself over to worldly life for several years. As an adult he returned to the faith and made a series of pilgrimages to shrines as a method of penance. Back in Siena, Italy, he joined the Dominicans, and died while caring for plague victims.
