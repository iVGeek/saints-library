Born to the Italian nobility, the daughter of Ildebrandino Cacciaconti. Married to Naddo di Benuccio Piccolomini, a rich nobleman from Corsignano, Italy. Widowed, she withdrew from worldly life to her home in Belsedere, Italy, and went out each day to care for the poor and encourage the faith in the people in the countryside. By the time of her death, she had spent herself into poverty caring for her neighbors.
On 6 May 1500, two Trequanda city councilors in a meeting witnessed a swarm of bees pouring in and out of a gap between the stones of the wall of the church of Saints Peter and Paul. Checking closer, it appeared that the bees had built their hive in the tomb of Bonizzella. The tomb was dismantled to clear the hive from the church; in the tomb they found no sign of a hive, but did find the incorrupt body of Bonizzela. Her burial clothes looked fresh, the body smelled of incense, and in her hands she held a honeycomb built by bees in the shape of a goblet. Understandably seeing this as a sign, devotion to Blessed Bonizzella began, and continues today.
