Lay woman, married to Antonio María Colomina Bignati in the archdiocese of Granada, Spain. Mother of four, two of whom died in infancy. Active in her , Carmen helped raise funds to rebuild the church after it was burned by Communists in 1932. She had personal devotion to the poorest in the town, especially fishermen. Her active faith led to her being a target of the anti–Church persecutions of the Spanish Civil War. She tried to hide in Madrid, Spain, but was caught, imprisoned, tortured and eventually executed. Martyr.
