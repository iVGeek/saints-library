Born to a wealthy and pious Christian family. Sister in the Daughters of Mary, Help of Christians, joining in Seville, Spain, and making her final profession in 1914. She served as a teacher in Sarria, Spain, director of the Valverde del Camino and Jerez houses, and then Provincial Vicar of the Daughters in Sarria. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
