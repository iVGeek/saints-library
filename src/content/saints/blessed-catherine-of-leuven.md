Born to a Jewish family, Rachel converted to Christianity, having learned about the faith from listening to her father argue theology with the chaplain of the Duke of Brabant (in modern Belgium).  She ran away from home to the Parcum Damarum (Parc-aux-Dames) abbey near Leuven (in modern Belgium) to receive Baptism, taking the name Catherine. Her family tried to get her to return home, even enlisting the support of local civil and Church authorities, leading to long, harsh legal and theological arguments, but Catherine preferred life in the convent, and became a Cistercian nun. Over the years, she received visions, and was known as a miracle worker.
