Ciriaco joined the Redemptorists at El Espino, Spain on 21 September 1904. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Cuenca, Spain on 29 July 1917. Missionary to Mexico from 1920 to 1926 when the anti-clerical attitude of the authorities led him to return to Spain where he served at the Sanctuary of Perpetual Help outside Madrid. Transferred to Cuenca, Spain in May 1935. Kidnapped and murdered by Communist militia in the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
