Grew up with an uncle who was a priest. Joined the Congregation of San Pedro ad Víncula in Barcelona, Spain in 1901. Ordained a priest in Burgos, Spain in 1914. Teacher. Superior of his house. Elected Councilor-General of the Congregation in 1931. Superior of the Sant Feliu de Llobregat convent in Barcelona in 1936. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
