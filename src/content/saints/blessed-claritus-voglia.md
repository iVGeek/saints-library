Born to the Italian nobility. Though he was early drawn to religious life, Claritus married a lady named Nicolosia; they apparently had no children, which hints at a celibate marriage. Around 1342, Claritus received a miraculous intervention by Saint Zenobius of Florence. In gratitude, he founded the Regina Coeli convent of Augustinian nuns in Florence, Italy; it's also known as the Chiarito convent. His wife entered the house as a nun, and Claritus stayed there as a servant the rest of his life.
