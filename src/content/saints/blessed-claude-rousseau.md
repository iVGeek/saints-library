The son of a famous lawyer, member of parliament, and professor of law at the University of Paris. Claude began studying law himself, but was drawn to religion and switched to studying theology at the Laon seminary in Paris. He joined the Sulpicians in 1774 while a deacon. Ordained a priest in 1775. Professor of moral theology at the Laon seminary. Arrested on 16 August 1792 for refusing to take the oath of loyalty to the government in the French Revolution. One of the Martyrs of September.
