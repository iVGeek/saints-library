One of the early Franciscan friars; he received the habit from Saint Francis of Assisi. After some time in a Franciscan hermitage, he 1216 he was sent with Blessed Bernard of Quintavalle and some other friars to spread Franciscan spirituality in Spain. He returned to Italy in 1219 to attend the general chapter of the Order, then returned to Spain with the Servant of God Giovanni Parenti. Late in life, Clement returned to Italy and spent his final days at the La Verna sanctuary.
