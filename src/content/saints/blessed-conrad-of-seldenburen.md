Born to the nobility, a member of the royal house of Seldenbüren, and apparently the last member of the family. He founded and endowed Engelberg Abbey at Unterwalden, Switzerland in 1120; legend says that Conrad was told by an angelic voice where to build the abbey. which led to the name Engelberg = Angel Mount. He became a lay-brother at Engelberg, and was a spiritual student of its first abbot, Blessed Adelelmo. Considered a martyr because he was killed on a trip to Zurich to defend the rights of the abbey.
