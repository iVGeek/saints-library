Dominican priest, making his profession in the Order on 8 July 1923, and ordained on 10 November 1929. He earned a doctorate in theology at the Angelicum in Rome, Italy, and then stayed on for a while to teach there. He returned to Valencia, Spain, served briefly there as a teacher, and was martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
