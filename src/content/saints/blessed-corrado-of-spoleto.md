Franciscan friar at the convent of Sarnano, Macerata, Italy. Feeling a call to a more intensely religious life within Franciscan spirituality, in 1290 Corrado received permission from Pope Celestine V to rigorously follow the example of Saint Francis, withdrawing from all community life and obedience to his superior.
