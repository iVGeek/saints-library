Lay woman in the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain. The daughter of Joaquin Valls and Francisca Espí, Crescensia was baptized at the age of one day, and went to a school run by the Sisters of Charity. A pious girl and young woman, over the years she was a member of the Daughters of Mary, the Apostleship of Prayer, the Saint Vincent de Paul Society, Catholic Action, and became a Carmelite tertiary. She helped support her family through her needlework, and spent her time caring for the sick and organizing charity for the poor. In the persecutions of the the Spanish Civil War, she and three of her sisters were imprisoned for their open Catholicism, and executed late that night. Martyr.
