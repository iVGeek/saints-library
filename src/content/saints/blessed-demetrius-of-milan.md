Soldier who fought in Hungary and in Albania. Returning to Italy, Demetrius had a conversion experience, and felt drawn to his faith and religious life. He became a Franciscan friar at the convent of Monteluco near Spoleto, Italy, then lived for over 50 years in a nearby cave as a penance for his early life.
