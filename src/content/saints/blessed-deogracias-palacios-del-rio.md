The sixth of seven children in his family, Deogracius was known as a good student, and joined the Augustinian Recollects at age 15. Missionary to Brazil in 1923. Ordained a priest in Brazil on 28 March 1925. Missionary to Argentina. He returned to Spain in 1933, and served his remaining three years as superior of the Augustian communities in Monachil and Motril in the archdiocese of Granada, Spain. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
