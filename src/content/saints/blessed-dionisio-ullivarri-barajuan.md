Baptized on the day of his birth. Salesian brother, entering the novitiate in Sarria, Barcelona, Spain, making his vows on 1 March 1901. Worked as a bookbinder for colleges in Barcelona, Madrid and Salamanca in Spain, and in Cuba. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
