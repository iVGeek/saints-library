After his father's death, Dodone was forced into an arranged marriage for the good of his family, but after a few years he gave in to a call to religious life, and entered the Premonstratensian abbey of Mariëngaarde (Garden of Mary) in Friesland near modern Hallum, Netherlands; both his wife and his mother became nuns at a nearby monastery. Dodone lived for a while in penance and isolation at Bakkeveen, Friesland, but his reputation for holiness spread, and his hermitage became a pilgrimage site of would-be spiritual students and people in need of miraculous healing. He spent some time preaching to the Frisians, exhorting them to give up their endless vengeful feuds and vendettas, and helping those who had been harmed in the fighting. In his latter years, he finally retired to live as a prayerful hermit at Haskerdijken, Friesland.
