Daughters of Charity of Saint Vincent de Paul nun in the Archdiocese of Madrid, Spain, joining in 19 May 1917. She served as a teacher and a nurse with her longest assignment being at the College of Mercy in Madrid, basically dealing with the infirmary. Superior of the Casa della Misericordia of Albacete. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
