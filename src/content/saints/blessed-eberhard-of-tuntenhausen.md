Shepherd who wandered the region of Freising, Germany who was known for his holy life, for his knowledge of herbal medicine, his ability to heal sick animals, and as a miracle worker. Legend says that when he would stick his shepherd's staff into the ground, a tree would grow from the spot; this may be an allegory for the useful and holy works he did as he travelled, and the good that came from it.
