The youngest of seven children born to the family of a poor shoemaker, Eduard was a carpenter's apprentice when he began feeling a call to the priesthood. Ordained a priest of the diocese of Osnabrück, Germany in 1940, he worked as an assistant pastor with Blessed Johannes Prassek and Blessed Hermann Lange at the Herz-Jesu parish in Lübeck, Germany. Father Eduard had little interest in politics, serving as a youth minister in the parish, but during World War II he would listen to radio broadcasts in English, he distributed printed sermons by Bishop Clemens August von Galen, and was opposed to the Nazi extermination of the physically and mentally ill. These actions and speech led to his imprisonment, with his brother priests and several lay people, in June 1943 for treason. Martyr.
