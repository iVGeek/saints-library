The youngest of 22 children born to José Pironio and Enriqueta Rosa Butazzoni, who had emigrated to Argentina from Italy. He studied for the priesthood at the seminary in La Plata, Argentina, and the Pontifical Angelicum Athenaeum in Rome, Italy. Ordained a priest for the diocese of Mercedes, Argentina on 5 December 1943. Chosen auxiliary bishop of La Plata, and titular bishop of Caeciri on 24 March 1964. President of the Latin American bishops' council. One of the Fathers of Vatican II sessions in 1964 and 1965. Chosen bishop of Mar del Plata, Argentina on 19 April 1972. Chosen Pro-Prefect of the Congregation for Religious and Secular Institutes, and titular archbishop of Thiges on 20 September 1975. Elevated to cardinal–deacon of Santi Cosma e Damiano on 24 May 1976 by Pope Paul VI, and chosen Prefect of the Congregation for Religious and Secular Institutes on 29 May 1976. Part of the August 1978 conclave that elected Pope John Paul I, and the October 1978 conclave that chose Pope John Paul II; Cardinal Eduardo had been considered for the papacy himself during those conclaves. Helped found World Youth Day. Cardinal–Priest of Santi Cosma e Damiano on 22 June 1987, and then Cardinal–Bishop of Sabina-Poggio Mirteto on 11 July 1995. He finally retired from his administrative and curial duties on 20 August 1996.
