Born to the Alemannic nobility, he was related to Hildegard, second wife of Blessed Charlemagne, and a served in the imperial court. Bishop of the diocese of Verona, Italy in 780. He built the Saints Peter and Paul convent on Reichenau Island (in modern Germany, and in 799, realizing that he no longer had imperial support, retired from his see and became a monk at that house for the remaining three years of his life.
