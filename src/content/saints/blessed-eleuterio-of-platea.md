A Mercedarian lay knight, noted for his personal piety and zeal for the austere life. In 1257 he and Blessed Ferdinand of Portalegre were sent to Algiers to ransom Christians enslaved by Muslim Moors. They were captured, robbed and executed by Saracen pirates. Martyr.
