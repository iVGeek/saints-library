The third of nine children born in a devout family; four of her siblings did not survive childhood. On 10 May 1911, ten-year-old Teodora, on the night before her First Communion, had a vision of Saint Thérèse of Lisieux who said the girl would grow to become a nun. Member of the Association of the Blessed Imelda Lambertini. Member of the Angelic Army of Saint Thomas Aquinas. She joined the Dominican tertiaries on 20 April 1914, taking the name Agnes, and making her profession on 14 May 1915. She joined the Discalced Carmelite nuns in Bari, Italy on 8 April 1920, taking the name Elia of San Clemente, making her profession on 11 February 1925, and spending the rest of ther list in cloister there, serving as sacristan.
