Son of a physician; brother of Blessed Juan Carbonell Molla. Entered the seminary in Valencia, Spain in 1890. Ordained a priest in the archdiocese of Valencia in 1893. Co-adjutor of El Salvador. Hospital and asylum director. Noted preacher. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
