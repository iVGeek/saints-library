Elizabeth studied with the Augustinian Sisters. At age 22, against the wishes of her family, she became engaged to a young man from Ferrara, Italy; she broke off the engagement just before the wedding. At age 27 she began teaching at an orphanage run by Franciscan tertiaries. Due to abuse from her superior there, she left and began caring for abandoned and neglected children at the Istituto degli Esposti in Padua, Italy. In 1829 she opened a home and school for abandoned children and the neglected elderly, founded the Franciscan Elizabethan Sisters (Elizabethines) to run it and teach, and began spreading such schools and supporting the poor around the region.
