Benedictine monk, probably training at the St Georgen monastery in the Black Forest (in modern Germany). Chosen second abbot of the Saint John the Evangelist double monastery in Mallersdorf, Bavaria (in modern Germany). His leadership of the house led to expansion of its importance in the region, its influence with other houses, and brought it under the direct protection of Pope Innocent II. Abbot Eppo supported a strict observance of the Benedictine Rule, and defended the life at the 1132 synod at Piacenza, Italy. In 1136 he dissolved the double monastery, moved the sisters to a house in Eitting, Bavaria, and led the men's house the rest of his life.
