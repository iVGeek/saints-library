Priest. Provost of the cathedral of Saint Paul in Münster, Germany. Chosen bishop of Münster, Germany in 1084; he served the remaining 13 years of his life. Erfo supported Henry IV in the investiture controversy. He built several churches and other works in the diocese, supported the Saint Mauritius monastery to the point that he was considered a second founder, and consecrated the cathedral in 1090. Pilgrim to the Holy Lands in 1091 and 1092. His death was universally mourned by the people of his diocese.
