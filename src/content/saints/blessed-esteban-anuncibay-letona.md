Member of the Brothers of the Christian Schools, joining at age 16, taking the habit on 28 June 1909, making his first vows on 31 October 1911, and his solemn profession on 25 July 1919 in Madrid, Spain. Director of schools in the Chiclana and then of Puente Vallecas, one of the poorest neighborhoods of Madrid; anti–Catholic Communists tried to burn the school down in 1931. Director of the school of San Fernando, near Cádiz, Spain. Director of the San Luis School in Seville, Spain. Imprisoned with several his religious brothers on 30 July 1936 in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
