Daughters of Charity of Saint Vincent de Paul nun in the Archdiocese of Madrid, Spain, joining on 9 August 1890. She served as a teacher in several schools, and was known for her dedication to the Rule and spiritual life of the Daughters. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
