Born to the nobility, the son of Count Adolf I of Berg and his wife Adelheid von Lauffen; he became the Count of Berg (in the modern Rhineland in Germany). However, he felt a call to religious life and gave up his position to become a Cistercian monk at Morimond Abbey in Parnoy-en-Bassigny (in modern France) in 1129. In 1133 he and his brother Adolf II gave their property in Altenberg, including a castle, to the Cistercians who eventually founded a monastery there. Chosen the founding abbot of Asolveroth monastery, which became the Georgenthal Abbey in Gotha, Thuringia (in modern Germany) in 1143.
