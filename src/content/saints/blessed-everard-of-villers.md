Cistercian lay brother at Villers abbey in Brabant (in modern Belgium). He was a farm worker, and part of the rural religious group in the Court-Saint-Etienne area that was associated with the abbey. Everard was known for his piety and strict adherence to the rule and discipline of his vows. 
Legend saw that he was so dedicated to the rule that during a period of silence he refused to answer questions put to him by a knight; this led to a severe beating, Everard never made a sound, and when the knight understood why, he was so moved that he had a conversion experience and soon joined the Cistercians himself. There are also tales that Everard's piety was such that domestic and wild animals would obey him, and he could get them to do things that helped around the farm.
