Educated at a Salesian boarding school, Felipe joined the Salesians, trained in Campello, Spain, and made his profession on 1 August 1930 in Girona, Spain. Teacher in Ciudadela, Spain. He began his studies for the priesthood in Madrid, Spain in 1936. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
