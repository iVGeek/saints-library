Born in Portugal, Ferdinand spent most of his life in the Castilian region of Spain, living in Toledo, Valladolid and Zaragoza. He joined the Mercedarians as a lay knight, and in 1257 he and Blessed Eleuterio of Platea were sent to Algiers to ransom Christians enslaved by Muslim Moors. They were captured, robbed and executed by Saracen pirates. Martyr.
