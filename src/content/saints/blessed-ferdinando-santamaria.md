The oldest of five children, Ferdinando's father ran a rope-making business. The boy was drawn to religious life from an early age, and was stressed when he had to spend time at work instead of in church. On 5 March 1899, less than 16 years old, he became a Passionist cleric and seminarian at Paliano, Italy. While in seminary, he was stricken with acute meningitis; he never slacked in study or prayer, and set an example of living a holy version of the life you are given.
