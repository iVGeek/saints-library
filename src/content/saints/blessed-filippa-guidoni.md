Born to the nobility of Arezzo, Italy. Benedictine Santucci (later Servents of Mary) nun. Spiritual student of the founder of the Santucci, Blessed Santuccia Terrebotti. Founded the Santa Maria de Valverde abbey in Arezzo, and served as its first abbess.
