Feeling a call to religious life from her youth, Fiorina first applied to join the Dorotee Sisters of Vicenza, but was not accepted due to health reasons. However, on 27 August 1896, she joined the Sisters of Saint Joseph Benedict Cottolengo (Cottolengo Sisters) in Bigolino, Italy. She made her profession on 6 January 1899, and served as cook in houses in Giaveno and Turin, Italy.
In 1905, Sister Maria Carola began working in the missions in Kenya, serving as a teacher, administrator and catechist. She was remembered as a loving mother and supportive sister to all, native Kenyans and fellow missionaries alike, and was eventually assigned to Tigania and chosen superior of the missionary sisters in the region. There her health began to fail, and she died on the trip back to Italy, the last of the Sisters to leave the missions.
