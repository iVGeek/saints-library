We have no details of his youth, but it was apparently spent in a pretty dissolute manner. Foulques came to the faith, spent some time in penance, studied theology in Paris, and became parish priest in Neuilly-sur-Marne, France in 1195. He became known for his personal piety and effective preaching, working in the areas of Paris, Normandy, Lisieux, Caen, Burgundy, Picardy and Flanders, bringing many in the region to the faith. Known as a miracle worker, and to heal by prayer, laying on of hands, and making the sign of the cross over the sick. He became so popular and in demand, crowds would tear up his clothes to keep as relics. Foulques preached against usury, against prostitution, against married, worldly and undisciplined priests, and in support of the fourth crusade.
