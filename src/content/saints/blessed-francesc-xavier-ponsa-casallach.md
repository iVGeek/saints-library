Member of Catholic Action Youth. Joined the Hospitallers of Saint John of God on 20 February 1935 and became a novice in the community of San Baudilio de Llobregat, Barcelona, Spain on 2 June 1936. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War less than three months later.
