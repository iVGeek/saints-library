Priest in the diocese of Jaén, Spain; he celebrated his first Mass on 1 January 1917 in his home town. Parish priest in Cañada Catena, Spain where he was known for his ministry to the poor. Established a night school for working adults. Returning to  Villanueva del Arzobispo, Spain, he served as chaplain of the Daughters of Christ the King and priest in the parish of San Andrés; he was again noted for his care for the poor and teaching of adults who had gone to work as children. Archpriest of Orcera, Spain on 12 August 1933. In addition to his parish, administrative and school work, Father Francisco was an artist who painted and sculpted images of Our Lady. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
