Joined the Eudists in 1756. Ordained in 1761. Taught in the seminaries of the French cities of Caen, Dol and Domfort. Co-adjutor general of the Eudists on 16 October 1782. Chosen confessor to King Louis XVI in April 1791. One of the Martyrs of September.
