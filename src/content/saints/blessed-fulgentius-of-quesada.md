Born to the Spanish nobility, Fulgentius became a Mercedarian friar. In 1282 he was assigned by Blessed Peter de Amer to Morocco where he was abused for his faith and his mission, but still managed to free 204 Christians who had been enslaved by Muslims. Known for his work, his piety, and as a miracle worker.
