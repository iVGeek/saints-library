After studying at the seminary of the archdiocese of Barcelona, Spain, Gaietà was ordained a priest on 3 May 1888. He served as chaplain at a home for the elderly for over 20 years, and refused to abandon his flock when the area was seized by anti–Christian Communist militia in the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
