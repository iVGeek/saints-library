Benedictine monk at the Saint-Èvre Abbey in Toul, France; he served as its abbot for about 20 years, starting in 1208, where he was noted for leadership and administrative skills. Chosen prince–bishop of Toul in 1228 when he was in his 70's, he was confirmed in the position in 1229 by Emperor Frederick II. Garin was reluctant to accept the position, believing himself too old and weak to handle the political and religious turmoil of the times. He served less than two full years, resigning in early 1230, ending his days as a prayerful monk back in Saint-Èvre Abbey.
