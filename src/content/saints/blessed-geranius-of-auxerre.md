Monk of the abbey in Soissons (in modern France). Chosen the 40th bishop of Auxerre, France on 21 December 909. Worked with Richard II of Burgundy to relieve the siege of the city of Auxerre. He died while working at the royal court in Soissons to obtain the return of confiscated Church lands.
