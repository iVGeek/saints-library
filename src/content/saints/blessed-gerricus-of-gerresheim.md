Born to the landed Frankish nobility; he controlled the city of Gerresheim, which is named for him. About the year 870, he founded the Saint Margaret's cenobitic monastery for noble women in Gerresheim, and his daughter became its first abbess; it was later known as the Gerresheim Abbey.
