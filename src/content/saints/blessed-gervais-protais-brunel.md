Trappist monk. Priest. Prior of La Trappe Abbey in early 1790. Imprisoned on the ship Deux-Associés in the harbor of Rochefort, France on 27 January 1794, and left to die during the anti–Catholic persecutions of the French Revolution for refusing to take the oath of loyalty to the civil government. One of the Martyrs of the Hulks of Rochefort.
