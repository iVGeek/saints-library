The son of Gregorio and Camilla Perti, Giambattista joined the Franciscan Friars Minor Conventual at Mondaino, Italy as a teenager in 1554. There he studied theology, earning a doctorate while still a young man. Priest. Assigned by Pope Pius V to serve as confessor to the soldiers of the Duke of Urbino, and thus he was at the battle of Lepanto on 7 October 1571.
Feeling a need for a more ascetic life, Father Giambattista travelled to Spain and joined the Discalced Friars Minor. He spent the years 1576 to 1587 as a travelling Alcantarine missionary and preacher. After 20 years on the roads, he returned to Italy and became a noted preacher, worked endlessly each Lent to bring people back to the faith. Founded several Alcantarine convents. Saint Philip Neri and Saint Felix of Cantalice knew him, and each considered Father Giambattista a walking saint.
