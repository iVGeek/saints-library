Born to the nobility, in a wealthy and powerful family, Giovanna felt a call to religious life and left the worldly one behind to become a Poor Clare nun. Spiritual student and one of 16 companions of Saint Catherine of Bologna who helped found the Corpus Domini monastery. Vicar of the Poor Clare house in Ferrara, Italy. Vicar of the house in Bologna, Italy. One of Saint Catherine's visions showed Giovanna sitting beside her in paradise.
