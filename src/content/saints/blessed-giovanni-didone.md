Giovanni grew up in the Paduan countryside, and early felt a call to a religious vocation. He studied at the diocesan seminary in Padua, and joined the Xaverian Missionaries in 1950, making his perpetual vows on 5 November 1954. After studies at Piacenza, Italy, he was ordained a priest on 9 November 1958. Missionary to Fizi, Democratic Republic of Congo, leaving on 3 December 1959. He was known for his strong devotion the Virgin Mary and his trust in the guidance of the Holy Spirit. Martyr.
