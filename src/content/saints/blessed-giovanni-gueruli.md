Deacon. Canon of the cathedral of Santa Columba in the diocese of Rimini, Italy. Vicar-general to Bishop Guido de Caminate in the diocese of Rimini in 1292. There are indications that he was an Augustinian, and that he was a Franciscan, but neither is certain. Known as role model for holy charity. Reported miracle worker. Appears to have received a vision of the Holy Face while in devotion before a cross.
