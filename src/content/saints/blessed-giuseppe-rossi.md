Born the second son of a poor family. After studying at the seminary in the diocese of Novara, Italy, Giuseppe was ordained a priest on 29 June 1937. Parish priest at the church of San Gottardo in the small mountain town of Castiglione Ossola, Italy in 1939. He served there through World War II, organizing Catholic Action and Saint Vincent de Paul Society groups in the parish.
On 25 February 1945 fighting broke out at Castiglione Ossola between fascists and Garibaldi's partisans. The fascists, part of the Black Brigades known for their virulent anti–Christianity, retaliated against the town residents, burning houses and kidnapping residents; Father Giuseppe was accused of ringing the church bells as a signal to the partisans. He and the other residents were released, but on the evening of the next day he was again siezed by the fascists, forced to dig his own grave, and then murdered. Martyr.
