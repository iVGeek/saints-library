Spiritual student of Saint Francis of Assisi in the early 13th century. One of the first Franciscan friar, joining at the Monte di Santa Catarina house near Urgezes, Portugal. His preaching and ministry made him a favourite with the faithful in nearby Guimarães, Portugal. There Gualter started what became a Franciscan convent, and used it as a base for his lifelong work. He was known for his piety, prayer life, charity, ministry to the sick and imprisoned, and as a spiritual leader of the local people.
