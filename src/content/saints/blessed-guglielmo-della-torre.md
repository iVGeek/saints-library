Born to the Swiss nobility, the son of Ruggero and Adilia. Priest. Canon of the cathedral of Como, Italy. Bishop of Como from 1197 until his death, serving nearly 30 years. Helped to smooth out some differences that had arisen between his diocese and the Vatican. Participated in the Fourth Lateran Council in 1215. Supported the call to the fifth Crusade, and travelled part of the way with the Crusaders. He had a good relationship with emperor Otto IV, which benefitted the diocese, but when Otto came into conflict with the Pope, Guglielmo stayed loyal to the Church to the point of refusing imperial orders. Brought the Dominicans into his diocese in 1219, helped them get established, and to oppose the Patarines, a proto-Protestant movement. Financed convents, churches and hospitals.
