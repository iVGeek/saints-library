The son of a fisherman, Gonçalo became an Augustinian friar at about age 20. He was renowned as a theologian and preacher, concentrating his catechesis work on children, the illiterate and uneducated. A gifted artist, he illuminated and illustrated liturgical books. Prior of the convent of Saint Lawrence in Miragaia, Lourinhã, Portugal in 1394. Superior of the convent of Our Lady of Grace in Lisbon, Portugal in 1404. Superior of the convent of Our Lady of Grace in Santarém, Portugal in 1408. Prior of the Convent of Our Lady of Grace in Torres Vedras, Portugal in 1412 where he served the rest of his life. In all these houses he worked to teach his brother friars, and did all the menial jobs as an example of humility and of Christ among them.
