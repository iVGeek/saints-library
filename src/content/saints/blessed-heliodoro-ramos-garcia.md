Baptized at the age of two days, Heliodoro was a religious boy and grew up with a call to religious life. He joined the Salesians of Don Bosco in Mohernando, Guadalajara, Spain, and made his solemn profession as a coadjutor lay brother on 23 July 1936. The same day, his house was raided by anti–Christian Communist forces in the Spanish Civil War, and the brothers imprisoned or dispersed. He spent several months imprisoned with brother Salesians, never gave up his faith, and was finally executed for it. Martyr.
