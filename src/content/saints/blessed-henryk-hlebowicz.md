> Henryk Hlebowicz (1 July 1904 – 9 November 1941) was a Polish Roman Catholic priest. Born in Grodno, he served as a parish priest and lecturer at Vilnius University between 1930 and 1935. After the outbreak of World War II, he engaged in pastoral work while also working with partisan organizations. He was executed at Barysaw in 1941, and was beatified as one of the 108 Martyrs of World War II in 1999.
