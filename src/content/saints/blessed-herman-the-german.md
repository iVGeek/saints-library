In 1218 he travelled from Krakow to Rome with Bishop Iwo Odrowaz, Saint Hyacinth and Blessed Ceslas Odrowaz where he met with Saint Dominic de Guzman and joined the Dominicans, receiving the habit from Saint Dominic himself. The new friars then returned to Silesia to spread Dominican thought and spirituality. On the way, Herman, Saint Hyacinth and Blessed Conrad of Frisach founded the convent of Fiesarch in Carinthia, and Herman stayed there as its superior.
