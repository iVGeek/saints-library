Born to the nobility of the Rhineland-Palatinate in modern Germany. Priest. Canon of the church of Saints Cassius and Florentius in Bonn. Dean of the church of the Holy Apostles in Cologne. Called to religious life, he joined the Cistercian abbey in Himmerod. He led the Cistercian brothers who took over an abandoned Augustian property and founded the Saint Peter Cistercian monastery there in Stromberg c.1195. Abbot of the Himmerod abbey. One of the founders, and the first abbot, of Marienstatt abbey in Streithausen, Westerwaldkreis in the Rhineland-Palatinate in 1212; legend says that he chose the location for the abbey based on a dream or vision in which the Blessed Virgin Mary showed him the location where a hawthorn bush suddenly flowered in winter. After serving as abbot there for over 20 years, he retired to Himmerod abbey, and spent his remaining years as a prayerful monk.
