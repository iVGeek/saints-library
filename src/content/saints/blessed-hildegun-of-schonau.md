Dressed as a boy for her own safety, Hildegun accompanied her father on a pilgrimage to the Holy Land. On her return home, she kept the male disguise, joined the Cistercian monks at Schönau, Germany, and lived the rest of her life as a brother, using the name Joseph. Legend describes her as a miracle worker, and her sex was discovered only on her death. Of all the old stories with this motif, and there are several, Hildegun's has the best and most convincing documentation by her own order.
