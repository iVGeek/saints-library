Member of the Diocesan Laborer Priests of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, joining on 12 August 1910 while still in seminary. Ordained a priest on 8 December 1912. He served and taught in seminaries in Mexico for a couple of years. Returning to Spain, he taught for a few months, then worked on the magazine El Correo Josefino. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
