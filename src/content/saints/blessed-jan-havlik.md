The eldest of four children in a very poor family. A member of the Society of Missionaries of Saint Vincent de Paul, Ján began studying at the seminary in Banska Bystrika, Slovakia in 1949. He was noted as an excellent speaker, an excellent singer, and for his devotion to the Virgin Mary. On 29 October 1951 he was arrested by the Communist authorities, imprisoned, starved, neglected, interrogated, put to forced labor, and repeatedly tortured before being sentenced to 10 years; the sentence was later extended to 11 years for refusing to renounce his faith. His health broken, he was finally released on 29 October 1962; he returned home and died soon after. Martyr.
