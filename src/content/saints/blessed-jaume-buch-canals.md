A Salesians brother, joining in Girona, Spain at age 14; he made his profession in Barcelona, Spain in 1908. Teacher in Valencia, Spain, popular with and admired by his students. He had a devotion to Mary, Help of Christians, and worked spread the devotions to his students and brothers. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
