Member of the Claretians, making his profession in Vich, Spain on 15 August 1930. On 1 July 1936 he entered the seminary in Barbastro, Spain where to study for the priesthood and where he served as choir master. On 20 July 1936 he had to enter hospital; on 15 August he was released and immediately imprisoned as part of the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War. Three days later he was executed. Martyr.
