Member of the Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of the Apostles, making her first vows on 8 September 1959 and taking the name Sister Angèle-Marie. She was asssigned to the Sisters's house in Bouzarea where they operated an orphanage and a boarding school for young girls. Sister Angèle taught embroidery from 1959 to 1964 at the school, and then from 1964 at the Algiers School of Art in Belcourt where she served the rest of her life. Martyred by Islamic forces.
