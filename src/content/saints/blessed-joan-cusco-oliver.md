One of eleven children born to a farm family. He studied at the school of San Ramón de Vilafranca del Penedés, was a good and pious student, and early felt a call to religious life. He joined the Sons of the Holy Family in 1891, an order devoted to supporting family life, and made his solemn vows on 16 October 1897. Ordained a priest, in the diocese of Vic, Spain on 23 September 1899. Teacher at and head of the San José de Tremp school in Lleida, Spain. Noted spiritual director and confessor. Imprisoned and martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
