The son of Mattia Boher and Benvenuta Foix. He studied in seminary of the diocese of Urgel, Spain, and was ordained a priest on 11 April 1914. He served as coadjutor in various parishes and in 1929 was appointed parish priest of Pobleta di Belvehí. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
