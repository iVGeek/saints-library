The son of José and Francisca Ferrer, the boy was baptized at the age of one day. He joined the Piarists on 7 August 1919; he made his solemn profession on 18 February 1925. Ordained a priest on 25 December 1926. He taught school in Albacete, Spain, and served as Piarist novice master in Albarracín, Spain. In the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War, Father José returned to his parents' house in Algemesí, Spain, but was seized by the Communist militia, transferred to Llombay, Spain, and executed for the crime of being a priest. Martyr.
