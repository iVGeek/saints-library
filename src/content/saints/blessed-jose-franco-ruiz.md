The eldest of 14 brothers, José was baptized at the age of one day and confirmed on 18 September 1910. He joined the Congregation of San Pedro ad Vincula on 22 May 1926, and taking his final vows on 24 September 1932. Served as a school teacher in Barcelona, Spain. Imprisoned and executed by militiamen in the Spanish Civil War.
