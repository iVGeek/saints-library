The son of Antonio Perot and Celestina Juanmartí, the family moved from France to Spain when José was still a young boy. He studied in the seminary of the diocese of Urgell, Spain, and was ordained a priest on 28 March 1903. Father José served as coadjutor in several parishes, and as parish priest of San Juan di Vinafrescal. Martyred in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War.
