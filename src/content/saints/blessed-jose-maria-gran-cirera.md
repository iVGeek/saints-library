A member of the Missionaries of the Sacred Heart, joining on 8 September 1966, and making his solemn profession on 8 September 1969. Ordained a priest in Valladolid, Spain on 9 June 1972. Served as parish priest in Valencia, Spain. In 1975 he was sent to minister in Guatemala where he worked with the poorest people until murdered with Blessed Domingo del Barrio-Batz by the military. Martyr.
