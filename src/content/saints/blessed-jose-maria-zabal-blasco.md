A lifelong layman in the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain. José's father died when the boy was 12 years old. He apprenticed in a law office, and a railroad managerial staff. Married to Catalina Cerdá Palop on 3 May 1925; the couple had three children. Member of Catholic Action and a Catholic trade union where he was known for defending workers' rights. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
