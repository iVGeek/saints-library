A lifelong layman in the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain, José was baptized on the day of his birth. He grew up in a farming family, was a member of the Catholic Agricultural Union and Catholic Action, and served as a catechist. He married Purificación Esteve Martínez on 29 January 1913, but the couple had no children. A few months into the Spanish Civil War, he hid two Discalced Carmelite brothers and a Cistercian nun in his home, but they were found; they and José were murdered. Martyr.
