Joined the Claretians in Cervera, Spain in 1927. Priest, ordained on 24 February 1934 in Valencia, Spain. Taught at the College of Játiva. Assigned to Calatayud and Barbastro to teach seminarians. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
