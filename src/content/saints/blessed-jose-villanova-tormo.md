Baptized at the age of two days, José joined the Salesians of Don Bosco in his late teens; he made his profession 25 July 1920 in Carabanchel Alto, Madrid, Spain. Ordained a priest in 1929. He served as a teacher in Salamanca and Madrid, Spain.  Martyred in the Spanish Civil War for the offense of being a priest.
