Studied at convent schools in Solsona and Calanda, Spain. He joined the Dominicans, and was professed in the Order in Valencia, Spain on 4 October 1929. Ordained a priest in Valencia on Holy Saturday in 1936. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
