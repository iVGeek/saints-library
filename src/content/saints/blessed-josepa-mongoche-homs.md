Professed religious in the Sisters of the Christian Doctrine. Worked as a seamstress to support the Sisters, and was known for her devotion to Mary; she could recite the work “The Glories of Mary” by Saint Alphonsus de Liguori. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
