Entered the Carmelites on 31 January 1911. Joined the Hospitallers of Saint John of God at age 30. Worked at an asylum hospital. Seized as he was leaving the hospital where he worked as a nurse to crippled children, he was ordered to blaspheme to prove that he renounced Christianity; he refused. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
