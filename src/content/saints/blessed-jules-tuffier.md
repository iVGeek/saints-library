After studying at the college of the Picpus Fathers, Jules joined the congregation, making his profession on 14 May 1823. Ordained a priest in 1830, he served as a parish priest, chaplain to convents, and superior of teaching Fathers at several colleges. Procurator and General Councilor of the Fathers. He was known for the depth of his preaching. Martyred in the persecutions of the Paris Commune.
