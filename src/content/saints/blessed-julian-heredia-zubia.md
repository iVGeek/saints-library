The son of Justo Heredia and Ceferina Zubía. On 29 October 1889, at the age of 15, Julian became a novice at the Cistercian monastery of Val San José in Getafe, Spain, taking the name Fray Maria Pio; he made his solemn profession on 27 May 1897. Ordained a priest on 18 March 1899, he served his house as cantor, sub-prior and taught theology. He transferred to the monastery of Santa María de Viaceli in Cóbreces, Spain on 2 February 1908 where he served many years as novice master. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War with several of his brother monks.
