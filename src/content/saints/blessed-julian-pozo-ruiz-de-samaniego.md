Julián entered the El Espino Redemptorist seminary as a boy, joined the Order/a> in his teens, and made his profession in 1920. He was diagnosed with tuberculosis in 1921. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Cuenca, Spain on 27 September 1925. His health prevented Father Julián from work as a missionary, so he concentrated on ministry as a confessor, spiritual director, and with working with the sick. Transferred to Cuenca in 1928. Arrested there by Communist militia in the early days of the Spanish Civil War; he was spotted praying the Rosary, and executed for the crime of being a priest. Martyr.
