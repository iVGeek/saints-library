The second of sixteen children born to Kaspar Stanggassiner and Crescencia Hamberger, the boy was baptized on the day he was born. Kaspar entered the junior seminary of Freising, Germany at age 10, and at the age of 16 made personal vows of chastity. A Marian pilgrimage in the summer of 1892 brought him to the decision to join the Redemptorists, and over his father's objections, he joined the Order in Gars, Germany. Ordained a priest on 16 June 1895 at age 24, Father Kaspar requested assignment to the missions in Brazil, was sent to the missionary school in Dürrnberg, Germany – and wound up a teacher and then its director. There he became known as an excellent teacher and spiritual director, and for helping form the vocations of many young people, leading by his example of prayerful and joyful Christianity.
