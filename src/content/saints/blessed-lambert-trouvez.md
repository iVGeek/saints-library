Richard worked as a tailor in Brussels, Belgium until he gave into a call to religious life and became a Franciscan Friars Minor (Observants) lay brother. Missionary to Mexico, then to the Philippines. Ordained a priest in Cebu, Philippines. Assigned to Japan in 1613 where he worked until the missionaries were outlawed and he was arrested and executed in the persecution of the Tokugawa Shogunate known as the Great Genna Martyrdom.
