Studied at the Pontifical Seminary Shkodrë, and theology in Innsbruck, Austria. Ordained on 24 April 1916 as a priest of the archdiocese of Shkodrë-Pult, Albania. Served in several mountain village parishes. Imprisoned, tortured and dumped into a sewage pit to die during the anti–Christian persecutions by the Albanian Communist government. Martyr.
