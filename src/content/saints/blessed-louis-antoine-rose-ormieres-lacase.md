Ordained a priest in the diocese of Carcassonne, France on 21 December 1833. Professor in his seminary while still studying. He founded the Congregation of the Guardian Angel Sisters, a teaching order with a mission to bring back the parish schools that had been destroyed by the anti–Christian persecutions of the French Revolution.
