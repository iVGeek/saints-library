Joined the Xaverian Missionaries in 1947, making his perpetual profession on 5 November 1959. Ordained a priest on 15 October 1961. Missionary to Baraka, Democratic Republic of Congo in 1962 where he was known for his humble service and deep prayer life. Martyr.
