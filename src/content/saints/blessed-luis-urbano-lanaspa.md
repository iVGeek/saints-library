Dominican priest, professed in the Order on 30 October 1899. He earned degrees in physics and theology, and taught for several years in Valencia, Spain. He wrote and served as editor several theology journals, was a much sought after preacher, and worked to alleviate some of the social problems in poor areas. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
