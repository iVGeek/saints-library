Cistercian nun at the Oberweimar abbey near Weimar, Thuringia (in modern Germany), joining at the age of 12. In poor health herself, Lukarda was assigned to the infirmary where she became known for her endlessly patient care for the sick, and eventually as a miraculous healer. She had a deep and mystical prayer life, developed a great devotion to the Virgin Mary, Saint John the Baptist and the Passion of Christ, received a vision of Jesus, and was imprinted with the wounds of the stigmata.
