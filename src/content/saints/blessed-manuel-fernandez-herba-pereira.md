Educated at Piarist schools in Celanova, Spain, and at the seminary in Tuy, Spain; he was noted for his aptitude in the hard sciences. He joined the Dominicans in Padrón, Spain on 20 November 1895, and then studied and served in Cuevas de Vera, Spain, and Caracas, Venezuela where he was chapel organist. Priest. He taught chemstry and French at the school in Almagro, Spain in 1918 and 1919, and then was chosen Provincial Prior in 1919 and 1935 and served in administration of the Order. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
