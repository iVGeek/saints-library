Layman in the archdiocese of Sevilla, Spain, he lived with his widowed mother. Member of the Society of Saint Vincent de Paul. Sacristan for the nearby Poor Clare convent in Sevilla. On 18 July 1936, at the start of the Spanish Civil War, he prevented an anti–Catholic mob from disrupting Mass at the convent. The next day, the 19th, he was seized and shot by the group; he survived 3 more days before dying of his wounds. Martyr.
