Baptized at the age of one day. As she grew, Margarita felt a call to religious life, and in 1896 became a novice in the Capuchin Sisters of the Mother of the Divine Shepherd; she made her solemn profession in 1903. She served as a teacher, known to her students as a excellent, patient educator and example of the joy of Franciscan life. Martyred in the early days of the Spanish Civil War.
