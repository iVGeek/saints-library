Daughter of Anacleto González and María del Rosario Trujillano, Juliana was baptized at the age of 11 days. Tertiary in the Franciscan Sisters of the Divine Shepherdess (now known as the Franciscan Missionaries of the Divine Motherhood) in 1900. She joined the Sisters at the Madrid house on 18 February 1903, making her solemn profession in 1910 in La Coruña, Spain. One of the founders of the Colegio de Arenas de San Pedro where she served as a teacher. She was later assigned to the motherhouse of the Missionaries, and served as a sacristan. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
