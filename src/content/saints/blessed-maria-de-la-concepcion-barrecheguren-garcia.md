Young lay woman of the archdiocese of Granada, Spain. Baptized at the age of 11 days. Her father, Venerable Francisco Barrecheguren Montagut, handled her religious education, and Conchita grew up very pious with a devotion to the Rosary, the Way of the Cross and Eucharistic adoration. She cared for the poor on her own, taught catechism, and worked with several groups with Marian devotions.
