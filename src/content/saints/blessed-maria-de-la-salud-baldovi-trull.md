The eldest of five children born to Juan Bautista Baldoví and Joaquina Trull. She joined the Cistercian nuns at the Gratia Dei Monastery in La Zaidía, Valencia, Spain in 1892, making her profession in 1893. She served as abbess of the house from 1917 to 1921. Feeling a call to a stricter observance of the Cistercian Rule, she and six like-minded sisters founded the Fons Saltus abbey in June 1925, and Mother María Micaela served as its first abbess. In June 1936, when the Spanish Civil War broke out, the sisters were kicked out of their house which was covert to a prison. On 16 October 1936, Mother Maria was imprisoned in her own abbey, and subjected to several weeks of abuse before finally being executed. Martyr.
