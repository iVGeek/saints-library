A lifelong lay woman of the archdiocese of Valencia, Spain, she was baptized on 1 January 1904, confirmed on 22 April 1909, and made her first Communion on 2 June 1912. She devoted her life to teaching working people. Imprisoned and murdered with her brother in the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
