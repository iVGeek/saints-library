Daughters of Charity of Saint Vincent de Paul nun in the Archdiocese of Madrid, Spain, joining on 2 December 1926. She served as a nurse in the Asylum of Malaga and in the psychiatric hospital of Santa Isabel de Leganés. She devoted herself completely to the mentally ill for whom she had a particular gift of understanding, affection and dedication. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
