The daughter of poor manorial servants and farmers in the archdiocese of Veszprém, Hungary, she was baptized at the age of one week. As Maria loved to read, her parish priest gave her access to a library of religious books, and she gave herself a deep religious education. She felt a call to religious life, but dedicated herself to caring for and catechising children and young people in her region. Murdered by a Russian soldier who planned to rape her. Martyr.
