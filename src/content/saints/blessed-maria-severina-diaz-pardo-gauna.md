Educated in a Carmelite school, Maria Severina's father was a member of the Society of Saint Vincent de Paul, and through them she encountered the Daughters of Charity; when she began to feel a call to religious life, she joined the Daughters in the Archdiocese of Madrid, Spain. Sister Maria Severina taught music in several schools over the next 19 years, finally at the College of the Immaculate Conception in Leganés. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
