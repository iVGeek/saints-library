Ordained a priest of the diocese of Cuneo, Italy on 19 June 1943. He was assigned to a parish in Boves, Italy, where he developed a ministry to young people suffering in World War II. chaplain to Alpine soldiers stationed nearby. Murdered by German troops as part of the Boves Massacre where civilians were killed in retaliation for an attack on the Germans; he had stayed to help children and the elderly escape, and was ministering to a dying man at the time. Martyr.
