Born to the Italian nobility, Martino became a career soldier and knight. In a moment of anger, he killed a friend and fellow knight in the area of Rimini, Italy. Martino fled and wandered aimlessly in grief until he came to the Benedictine convent of Capo Faro in Genoa, Italy. The brothers welcomed him in as a penitent, and he became a hermit in a cave in a grotto in the bay of Castelluccio in Pegli, Italy. He was known for his penitence, his humility, and for his charity to travellers; he was especially noted for repairing the clothes of pilgrims.
