Mathurin graduated university with degrees in literature and philosophy in 1748. Ordained a priest of the archdiocese of Paris, France in December 1753, Father Mathurin served in the Saint Gervase parish, and as chaplain to the local house of the Daughters of Charity. He earned a doctorate in theology in 1762. During the French Revolution, he was ordered by the authorities to take the oath of loyalty or stop his priestly duties; Mathurin refused to take the oath, but since the Daughters would not celebrate Mass with a priest who had taken it, he continued his work as their chaplain. For this he was imprisoned and then murdered in the street by an anti–Christian mob. One of the Martyrs of September.
