Born to the Italian nobility. He joined the Franciscans at the convent of San Nazaro in Novara, Italy, receiving the habit from Saint Bernardine of Siena. He became a famous preacher, always trying to model his work on Saint Bernardine, and with a noted devotion to the holy names of Jesus and Mary.
