Ordained a priest of the archdiocese of Freiburg im Breisgau, Germany on 5 July 1911. Writer, poet, and from 1921 to 1924 served as editor of the Esperanto magazine Katolika Mondo. Founded the Secular Institute Societas Christi Regis (Christ the King Society) charitable organization in 1926. Founded the Una Sancta Brotherhood in 1938, a group devoted to re-unification of the Lutheran and Catholic churches. Arrested several times by the Gestapo for preaching and writing about peace and pacifism, and advocating that Germany work for world peace. Executed in the Nazi persecutions.
