Born to a farm family, the son of Jan and Marianna née Wójcik. Having studied in the seminary of Kraków, Poland, Michal was ordained a priest on 1 February 1931. He continued studying theology at Jagiellonian University, and served as parish priest in Ploki, Poland for two years, then in Rajcza, Poland for four, and then back to Ploki in 1937. His pastoral work was limited during the Word War II occupation of Poland by Nazis. After the War the area came under the control of the Soviet Union, which began working to eradiate the Church. On the night of 11 May 1946, a group of 20 armed Stalinists kidnapped Father Michal, took him to a nearby wooded area, beat him, and then murdered him. Martyr.
