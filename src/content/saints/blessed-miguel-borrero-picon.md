Ordained a priest of the archdiocese of Sevilla, Spain on 19 November 1903. Assistant pastor of Utrera on 26 February 1923. Imprisoned by anti–Catholic forces on 19 July 1936 at the start of the Spanish Civil War; he survived a week in prison. Martyr.
