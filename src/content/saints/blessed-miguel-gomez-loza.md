Born to a peasant family, his father died when Miguel was very young. He was a pious boy, promoted the social doctrine of the Church, and was a member of the Catholic Association of Mexican Youth. He was married to María Guadalupe Sánchez Barragán in 1922, and the couple had three children.
Miguel became a lawyer and concentrated on defending the rights of the poor. The authorities did not care for this, had him arrested 59 times on various charges, and thugs beat him up even more. Beginning in 1927 he joined a group defending religious freedom against the anti-Church forces of the Mexican Revolution. Martyr.
