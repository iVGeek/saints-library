Miguel joined the Redemptorists on 8 September 1918, making his profession on 26 August 1920. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Cuenca, Spain on 27 September 1925. He served in the Spanish cities of Nava del Rey, Granada, Santander, Vigo, and Cuenca. Murdered by Communist anti–Catholic militia in the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
