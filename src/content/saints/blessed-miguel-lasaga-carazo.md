Baptized at the age of one day, Miguel joined the Salesians of Don Bosco in his late teens, making his solemn profession at Carabanchel Alto, Madrid, Spain on 31 July 1912. Ordained a priest in Barcelona, Spain on 21 May 1921. He supervised novice training and teachers in Italy, Peru and Spain. Imprisoned in July 1936 in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War, Father Miguel was known for ministering to other prisoners, including brother Salesians. Though many prisoners were released, Miguel was one executed for the crime of being a priest. Martyr.
