Studied at the seminary at Cuenca, Spain. Ordained a priest on 21 December 1901. Joined the Hermandad de Sacerdotes Operarios Diocesanos (Diocesan Laborer Priests of the Sacred Heart of Jesus) on 12 August 1903. He earned a decree in Canon Law at the seminary of Toledo, Spain. Prefect of the seminary in Toledo. Prefect of the seminary in Badajoz, Spain. Prefect of the seminary in Cuernavaca, Mexico. Prefect of the seminary in Querétaro, Mexico. Administrator of the seminary in Astorga, Spain. Spiritual director of the seminary in Valladolid, Spain. Spiritual director of the seminary in Salamanca, Spain. Spiritual director of the seminary in León, Spain in 1935. At the outbreak of the Spanish Civil War, he began a ministry in hiding, taking Communion to covert Catholics. Arrested by anti–Catholic forces on 9 April 1938, he was moved from prison to prison, beaten and tortured in one place after another, ministering to other prisoners until his health finally collapsed. Martyr.
