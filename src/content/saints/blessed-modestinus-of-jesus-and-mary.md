Born to a poor, working-class family; his father prepared hemp for use in rope making, and his mother worked as a weaver. An active and enthusiastic altar boy, Dominic early felt a call to religious life and began studying at the seminary in Aversa, Italy at age 18. He joined the Franciscan Friars Minor Alcantarini at Grumo Nevano, Italy in 1822, taking the name Modestinus of Jesus and Mary, and began his novitiate at the Santa Lucia convent in Naples, Italy. He made his profession on 27 November 1824, began his studies in philosophy and theology, and was ordained a priest on 22 December 1827 in Aversa. 
Father Modestinus threw himself into his vocation and became a well-known preacher, sought after confessor and spiritual director, and developed ministries to prisoners, the sick, and women about to give birth and needing extra help with the baby. He always carried an image of Our Lady of Good Counsel, to which he had been devoted from his youth, and his enthusiasm for his vocation led to a more active faith in many young people. Superior of the friaries of Mirabella Eclano in Avellino, Italy and Pignataro Maggiore in Caserta, Italy. Spiritual advisor to Blessed Pope Pius IX, to King Ferdinand II of Bourbon, and to an unknown number of fellow Franciscans and parishioners. In 1839 he was transferred to Santa Maria della Sanita in a crowded Naples slum; he lived there the remaining 15 years, working with the poor, the sick, and helping support families with newborns. He died of cholera contracted by working with the sick during a cholera epidemic.
