The third of seven children born to Francisco García and Joaquina Martí, Modesto was baptized at the age of two days. He studied at the Capuchin seminary in Massamagrell, Spain, took the habit on 1 January 1896, and made his perpetual vows as a friar on 6 January 1900. After additional studies, he was ordained a priest on 19 December 1903. Missionary in the area of Bogotá, Colombia. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
