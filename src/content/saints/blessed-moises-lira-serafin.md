Born to a pious family, the youngest of six children; his mother died when the boy was four years old. His father worked as a teacher, and the family was forced to moved regularly as the job required it. Priest. Member of the Missionaries of the Holy Spirit. Founded the Missionaries of Charity of Mary Immaculate.
