Cruciferi friar. One of the earliest spiritual students of Saint Francis of Assisi, he is reported to have been the fifth member of the Franciscans, and he attended the celebration of the canonization of Saint Francis. The preaching of Moricus brought others to the Order, including Blessed Ambrose of Massa.
