Ndoc studied in Albania and at the Jesuit Canisianum College in Innsbruck, Austria. He was ordained a priest in the archdiocese of Shkodrë-Pult, Albania on 21 September 1911. Arrested for his faith by Communists in Shkodrë on 8 December 1946, he was imprisoned at hard labour until his health failed; as he had become a burden, he was released. Father Ndoc never recovered, and though he died of natual causes months later, he is considered a martyr.
