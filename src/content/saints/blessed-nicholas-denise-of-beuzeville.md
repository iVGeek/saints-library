A well-educated priest. Canon of the cathedral, and vicar-general of the diocese of Coutance, France. Feeling a call to religious life, Father Nicholas resigned his positions and joined the Franciscans at the convent of Valognes in northwest France. Vicar of the Franciscan province of Paris, France. He opposed the proposed re-unification of the branches of the Franciscan Order as each had its own approach to Franciscan spirituality. Father Nicholas became a noted preacher in the region, and many of his sermons and theological writings have survived. He spent the latter years of his life as Guardian of the Franciscan convent of Rouen, France.
