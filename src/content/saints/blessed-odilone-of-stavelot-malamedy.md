Archdeacon in the bishopric of Verdun (in modern France). Monk at Gorze Abbey in 933 where he helped with its restoration by Saint Aldalbero of Metz. Abbot of the double monastery of Stavelot and Malamédy, in modern Belgium, from 936 to 954. He helped lead the house to being an important center in Cluniac reform movement, and when they were nearly obliterated by invading pagan Normans, he led the re-building work, and brought them back to material prosperity and spiritual leadership. He served in the Council of Verdun, and defended the rights of his houses against civil authority in several assemblies.
