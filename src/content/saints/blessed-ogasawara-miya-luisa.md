Lifelong lay woman in the diocese of Fukuoka, Japan. Married to Blessed Ogasawara Yosaburo Gen'ya. Mother of Blessed Ogasawara Genpachi, Blessed Ogasawara Mari, Blessed Ogasawara Kuri, Blessed Ogasawara Sasaemon, Blessed Ogasawara Sayuemon, Blessed Ogasawara Shiro, Blessed Ogasawara Goro, Blessed Ogasawara Tsuchi, and Blessed Ogasawara Gonnosuke. Martyred with her family and household servants.
