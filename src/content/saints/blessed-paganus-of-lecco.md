Educated in the Dominican convents in Bergamo and Rimini, Italy, Paganus was a Dominican friar for 50 years, received into the Order by Saint Dominic himself. He succeeded Saint Peter the Martyr as inquisitor general, serving in the Italian regions of Lombardy, Piedmont and Liguoria. He was murdered with two court notaries and two guards by accomplices of the heretical noble Corrado di Venosta with whom Paganus was in public conflict. Martyr.
