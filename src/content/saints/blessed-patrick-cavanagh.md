  - name: Novena to Blessed Blessed Patrick Cavanagh

      Novena — March 8 (9 days before feast day), nine days before the feast of Blessed Patrick Cavanagh (March 17).

    text: >-
      **Day 1**


      O blessed Patrick, who brought the light of faith to Ireland and labored with such love for the salvation of
      souls, intercede for me and my family.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 2**


      O blessed Patrick, who endured slavery and hardship with patience and trust in God, help me to bear my crosses
      with courage and to trust in God's providence.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 3**


      O blessed Patrick, who drove the snakes from Ireland as a symbol of driving out sin and evil, help me to rid my
      heart of all sin and to live a life of holiness.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 4**


      O blessed Patrick, who used the shamrock to explain the Holy Trinity, deepen my faith and understanding of the
      mysteries of our Catholic faith.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 5**


      O blessed Patrick, who ordained many priests and established the Church in Ireland, pray for holy vocations to the
      priesthood and religious life.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 6**


      O blessed Patrick, who wrote in your Confessio about your deep love for God, inflame my heart with a greater love
      for God and for my neighbor.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 7**


      O blessed Patrick, who faced many dangers and persecutions in your missionary work, give me courage to share my
      faith with others even in difficult circumstances.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 8**


      O blessed Patrick, who is known as the Apostle of Ireland, pray for the Irish people and for all Irish descendants
      throughout the world. May they remain faithful to their Catholic heritage.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Day 9**


      O blessed Patrick, who died in Ireland around 461 AD, obtain for me the grace of a holy death and the joy of
      seeing God face to face in heaven.


      (State your intention)


      Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be. St. Patrick, pray for us.


      ---


      **Closing Prayer**


      Thank you for joining in this novena. May the intercession of the saints strengthen your faith and bring you
      closer to God. Through their prayers and example, may you find peace, hope, and grace in your daily life.


      Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall
      be, world without end. Amen.


      Saints of God, pray for us.
