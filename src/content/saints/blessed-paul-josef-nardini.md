The son of Margherita Lichtenberger, a single mother; at age two he was adopted by his aunt, Barbara Nardini, and grew up as her son. An exceptional student, Paul studied in Speyer and Munich in Germany, graduating in 1846; his graduate thesis concerned “Demons in the New Testament”. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Speyer, Germany on 22 August 1846. He served briefly as a parish priest in Frankenthal, Germany. Prefect of the bishop's residence and spiritual director of several parishes and organizations in Speyer from 1 December 1846 till 11 January 1850. Parish priest in Primasen, Germany in 1851, an area of dispute and conflict between Catholics and Protestants.  Founded the Congregation of Franciscan Sisters of the Holy Family on 2 March 1855; the Sisters were devoted to the poor, sick, and children in the area, all of whom suffered the worst in the conflicts, and they continue their good work today in Germany, Romania, and South Africa.
