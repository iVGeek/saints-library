Ordained a priest of the archdiocese of Sevilla, Spain on 18 December 1910. Parish priest of Santa Maria de Guadalcanal on 15 October 1919. Arrested in his parish on 20 July 1936, imprisoned for several weeks, and then executed in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War. Martyr.
