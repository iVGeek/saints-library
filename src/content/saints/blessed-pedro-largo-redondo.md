Born to a peasant family. Joined the Passionists in 1922 in Corella, Spain. Never a great student, and known for a quick temper, his call to the priesthood was strong, and he was ordained in 1932 in Zaragoza, Spain. Assigned to serve as a military chaplain. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
