The son of Biagio Martret and Teresa Moles, Pedro entered the the junior seminary of the diocese of Urgell, Spain at age eleven. He was ordained a priest on 6 June 1925, and served as coadjutor in Puigcerda, then Organá, then Trem; treasurer of the church in Pobla di Segur in 1931. He and Father Silvestro Arnau were arrested together on 23 July 1936 in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War. He was imprisoned for several weeks, and then executed for the crime of being a priest.
