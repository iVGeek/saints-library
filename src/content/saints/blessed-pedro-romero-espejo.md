After studying at the Redemptorist in El Espino, Pedro joined the Order himself, and made his profession on 24 September 1880. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Cuenca, Spain on 29 February 1896. As he was a very shy person, Father Pedro devoted himself to a life of prayer and meditation, and a ministry of reconciliation in the Spanish cities of Astorga, Madrid and Cuenca. Detained several times by anti–Catholic Communist militia in the Spanish Civil War, he was finally imprisoned for his faith; he spent his time in prison ministering to other prisoners. Martyr.
