Pere was drawn to religious life from an early age, and he joined the Sons of the Holy Family in his late teens, making his solemn profession on 16 August 1903. Ordained priest on 19 September 1914. He taught science at the school of San José de Tremp, and then taught at the seminary of the Holy Family in Barcelona, Spain. Known as a highly educated and talented man with a great skill in communicating to students and creating enthusiasm for the topics he taught. Imprisoned and executed in the Spanish Civil War.
