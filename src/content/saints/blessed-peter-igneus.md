Born to the Florentine nobility, he was the uncle of Saint Bernardo degli Uberti, and related to Saint John Gualbert. Peter became at Vallombrosan Benedictine monk in 1018. He was accused of simony, and underwent a trial by fire – he had to walk through fire, and if he was innocent and God was on his side, he would emerge unharmed. Peter's abbot and spiritual teacher, Saint John Gualbert administered the test; Peter survived, was exonerated, and was know thereafter as Peter Igneus (Peter of the Fire). Abbot of San Salvatore Abbey in Fucecchio, Italy. Cardinal–bishop of Albano. Papal legate to Germany for Pope Saint Gregory VII in 1079. Papal envoy to France in 1084 to promulgate the order of excommunication against Henry IV.
