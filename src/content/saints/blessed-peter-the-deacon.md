Benedictine monk of the Sant'Andrea al Celio monastery in Rome, Italy. Friend and fellow student of Pope Saint Gregory the Great. Ordained a deacon, Peter assisted and travelled with Pope Gregory, served as his representative in Sicily and in Campania, and insured that Gregory's writings were preserved. Peter would have been considered a cardinal, if the position had existed at the time; this led to later depictions of him in cardinal's robes.
