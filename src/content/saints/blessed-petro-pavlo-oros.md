Son of a Greek Catholic priest. In 1919 the family moved to Transcarpathia. Petr studied in the diocesan seminary in Užgorod. Ordained a priest of the eparchy of Mukachevo, Ukraine, Ruthenian Rite, on 26 June 1942. Parish priest in Bol'šie Komjaty. Consecrated auxiliary bishop of Mukachevo on 19 December 1944. In 1948 the local government tried to get Bishop Petr to move to the Orthodox Church of the Moscow Partiarchate, but he refused. Murdered by a Soviet supporting militiaman. Martyr.
