Priest in the archdiocese of Paris, France. He served in the parish of St-Nicolas-des-Champes in Paris from 1784 to 1785, then as vicar of the parish of Saint-Merry. During the French Revolution, Father Pierre refused to take the oath of loyalty to the civil administration, and was arrested on 10 August 1792; he was the only priest in the prisoners of St-Germain-des-Prés Abbey, and spent his remaining time ministering to the others. One of the Martyrs of September.
