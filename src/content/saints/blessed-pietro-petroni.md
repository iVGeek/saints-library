Pietro showed great personal piety as a boy, and in his teens devoted himself to the care of sick people, especially lepers. Following a call to religious life, and against the wishes of his family, Pietro joined the Carthusians at the Maggiani charter house near Florence, Italy in 1328 when he was 17. The prior of the house saw Pietro as a candidate for the priesthood, but the young man saw himself as unworthy, and cut off the index finger of his left hand to make himself technically unqualified for ordination; it convinced everyone to drop the idea. He was known for his personal piety, as a miracle worker, and a visionary; his fame attracted so many would-be spiritual students, pilgrims and gawkers that Pietro, to retain the quiet, contemplative life for his brother monks, retired from the public altogether. His Life was written by Blessed Giovanni Colombini.
