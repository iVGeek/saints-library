A member of the Servants of Mary in Bologna, Italy, Piriteo became a travelling evangelist, preaching against heresy in Bohemia, repeatedly imprisoned for his work, and just as repeatedly freed by an angel. He moved on east, preaching Christianity in the Tartary region of central Asia until he was executed for his faith. Martyr.
