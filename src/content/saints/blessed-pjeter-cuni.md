Studied at the Pontifical Seminary in Shkodrë, Albania, and theology at the Propaganda Fide Congregation in Rome, Italy. Ordained on 23 March 1940 as a priest of the archdiocese of Shkodrë-Pult, Albania. Served in several mountain village parishes. Imprisoned, tortured, and dumped into a sewage pit to die during the anti–Christian persecutions of the Albanian Communist government. Martyr.
