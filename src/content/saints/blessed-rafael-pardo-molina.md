Dominican co-operator brother, making his profession on 2 January 1926. He served as a sacristan; at the outbreak of the Spanish Civil War, he worked to hide and protect sacred images, relics and vessels in the churches he cared for to keep them from being destroyed by the anti–Christian forces. Martyr.
