Raffaele travelled from his native Cattaro to Barletta, Italy to join the Servants of Mary. He spent his days begging to support his house, and his nights in Eucharistic adoration; he gave away much of the begged food and alms to the poor and it never made it back to the convent, and he spent so much time in work and prayer that he didn't even have a cell or bed assigned to him.
On 14 July 1566, while asleep in front of the Eucharist, Brother Raffaele had a dream in which he was told of his imminent death. He quickly woke up a superior who heard his confession and gave him Communion. He then went back to prayer in the church – and quietly passed away.
