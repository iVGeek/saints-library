Ramona joined the Capuchin Sisters of the Mother of the Divine Shepherd in April 1893 in the Sants area of Barcelona, Spain, and made her profession on 13 December 1899 in Igualada, Spain. She was known among the Sisters and her students as a cheerful, enthusiastic, hard-working and giving teacher, with a devotion to Mary and deep prayer life. Martyred early in the Spanish Civil War.
