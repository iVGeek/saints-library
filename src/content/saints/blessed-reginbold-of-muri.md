Benedictine monk at the monastery of Einsiedeln, Switzerland. Leader of the brothers sent to establish the abbey in Muri, Aargau, Switzerland in 1032; he served as provost for the remaining 23 years of his life. Reginbold worked tirelessly to establish the financial stability of the house, and to support the religious zeal and adherance to the Benedictine Rule by its brothers.
