Reinaldo joined the Augustinian Canons Regular convent of Saint-Jean-des-Vignes in Soissons, France while still a young man. Feeling drawn to a solitary life, Reinaldo became a holy hermit in the mountains of Craon, France and a spiritual student of Blessed Robert of Arbrissel who led several mountain hermits and canons. When Blessed Robert left the group, Reinaldo moved to the Mélinais forest near La Flèche in the diocese of Angers, France. There he built a chapel dedicated to Saint John the Evangelist; his reputation attracted many would be spiritual students.
