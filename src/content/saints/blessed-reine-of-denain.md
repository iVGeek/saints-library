Born to the 8th century nobility. Married to Count Blessed Adalbert of Ostrevant. Mother of ten daughters including Blessed Ragenfreda. Reine and Adalbert founded the Benedictine Denain Abbey; Ragenfreda served as its abbess, and when Adalbert died, Reine entered as a nun.
