Joined the Salesians in Bujedo, Spain in 1916 at the age of 13; made his perpetual vows on 26 August 1928. Teacher in Griñon, Spain. Director of the Salesian community of Santa Cruz de Mudela in 1935. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
