Born to the nobility, the son of Pandolph Malatesta, the Lord of Rimini, Robert was orphaned as a boy, and was educated in the home of his uncle. Robert was known as a pious youth who ignored the pleasures of his position, and spent much time in prayer, penance and works of charity. At age 16, he was married for political reasons to Margherita d'Este of the house of the Lord of Ferrara. Margherita was as drawn to religious life as Robert, and the two lived liked brother and sister, praying and working with the poor and sick. Robert became a Franciscan tertiary, and when he inherited the family position and wealth, spent largely caring for the poor and sick. He died a very young man, mourned by the many people he had helped.
