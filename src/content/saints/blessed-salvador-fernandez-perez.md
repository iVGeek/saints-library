Baptized on the day of his birth, Salvador grew to be a Salesian priest, making his profession in Sarrià-Barcelona, Spain on 8 December 1891, and being ordained on 19 September 1896. Known as a jovial, enthusiastic man who was willing to endlessly work at his vocation. Imprisoned, abused and finally executed in the Spanish Civil War for the offense of being a priest. Martyr.
