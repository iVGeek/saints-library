Born a princess, the daughter of King James II of Majorca and Queen Esclaramunda de Foix. She married Robert of Anjou on 21 June 1304; they had no children. She became queen of Naples on 5 May 1309 when Robert inherited the crown, and built the Saint Clare convent there. Widowed on 19 January 1343, she had Robert buried at the convent church, abandoned her royal status, and on 20 January 1344 entered the house as a Poor Clare nun, taking the name Clare, and living there her remaining year and a half.
