Seventh of the nine children born to Onofre Mestre and Mercedes Iborra, Santiago was baptized at the age of two days. A pious boy and early drawn to religious life, he joined the Capuchins at age 12, and made his perpetual profession in Rome, Italy on 21 April 1930. Ordained a priest in Rome on 26 March 1932. Father Santiago earned a degree in theology from the Gregorian University, and served as deputy director of the seminary in Massamagrell, Spain. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
