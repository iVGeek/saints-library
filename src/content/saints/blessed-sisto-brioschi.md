At age 16, after hearing a sermon by Saint Bernardino of Siena, Sisto joined the Franciscan Friars Minor at the convent of Sant'Angelo in Milan, Italy. Ordained a priest, Sisto was assigned to the convent of San Francesco in Mantua, Italy; it remained his base of operations for the rest of his life. He worked to live by the Franciscan Rule, and thus became a noted spiritual director, including to Blessed Bernardine of Feltre. Advisor on civil matters to Ludovico Gonzaga, Marquis of Mantua. Active supported the 1481 Crusade called by Pope Sixtus IV. In addition to his administrative and spiritual duties, Brother Sisto was known for his charity to the poor, begging in the streets for food, money and fuel to help the elderly, nursing the sick and visiting prisoners.
