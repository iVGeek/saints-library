Member of the Carmelite Sisters of Charity, beginning novitiate on 21 April 1900. Humble and very quiet, she served as a teacher in the Spanish cities of Manresa, Denia and Oliva, and then at the orphange of Cullera. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War.
