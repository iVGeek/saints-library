A 15th century member of the Servants of Mary, Tommaso lived for many years in the hermitage of Monte Granaro in Pesaro, Italy with Blessed Paolo Bigoni and Blessed Bonaventura Tornielli. Tommaso was known for his intense devotion to Mary, the depth of his prayer life, his penances and fasting, and as an enthusiastic and effective preacher.
