Born to the Spanish nobility, the daughter of Sancho García, Count of Castile, and Donna Urraca. Trigidia early felt a call to religious life, and c.1011, while still in her teens, she became a Benedictine nun at the double monastery of San Salvador in Oña, Spain, a house founded by her father and led by her aunt, the abbess Oñeca. She later served as the second abbess of the house. There are inscriptions around the abbey, and evidence from her burial, that she was known for her holiness and as a miracle worker, but no details of her life or service have survived.
