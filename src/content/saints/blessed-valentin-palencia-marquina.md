Priest in the archdiocese of Burgos, Spain known for his ministry to the poor and abandoned. Founded the Patronato di San Giuseppe, a group home for neglected boys with a work and spiritual teaching ministry to boys and men. Martyred with several of his parishioners in the Spanish Civil War. One of the Martyrs of Suances.
