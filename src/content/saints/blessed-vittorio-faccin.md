Vittorio grew up in the countryside of Modena, Italy. He joined the Xaverian Missionaries in 1950 at age 16, making his solemn profession as a lay brother on 8 December 1962. Assigned to Baraka, Fizi, Sud-Kivu, Democratic Republic of Congo in December 1959, working with the sick and displaced in areas in the middle a civil war. Martyr.
