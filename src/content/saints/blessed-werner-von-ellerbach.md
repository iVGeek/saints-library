Benedictine monk at the Saint Blaise Abbey in the Black Forest area of Baden-Württemberg in modern Germany. He and several brother monks were sent to establish the Wiblingen Abbey near Ulm, Germany in 1099; Werner served over 25 years as the first abbot.
