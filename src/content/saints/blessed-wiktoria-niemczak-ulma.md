Lay woman in the archdiocese of Przemysl, Poland. She married to Blessed Józef Ulma on 7 July 1935 and became dedicated to her home and children, Antoni, Barbara, Franciszek, Maria, Stanislawa, and Wladyslaw; a seventh child was on the way, but we have no record that they had chosen a name. She was active in parish life, and the local amateur theater group. Member of the Confraternity of the Living Rosary and the “Wici” Rural Youth Union. Executed by Nazi forces because her family hid Jewish refugees during World War II. Martyr.
