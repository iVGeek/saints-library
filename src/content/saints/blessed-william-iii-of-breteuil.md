Benedictine monk. Abbot of Breteuil in from 1105 until his death, the 3rd William to hold the office; he served for 26 years. He re-built the house after it had been nearly destroyed by Norman invaders. Served at the Council of Amiens in 1105, and the Council of Reims in 1119.
