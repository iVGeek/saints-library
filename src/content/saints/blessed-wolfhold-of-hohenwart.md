Priest. Confessor to Hohenwart Abbey. He was known to spend nights in the monastery church; legend says that the church doors would open for him on their own when he arrived, and close when he left, an action ascribed to angels.
Around the year 1492, a tailor named Stephen who lived in the Hohenwart area was suffering from gall stones, and was scheduled for 15th century surgery the next day. Stephen prayed for Wolfhold's intercession. The saint appeared to him and agreed to help. When Stephen woke, he was pain-free, and the gall-stones were gripped in his right hand.
