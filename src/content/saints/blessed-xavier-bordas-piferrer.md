Born to a pious family and educated by the Salesians, Xavier joined the Society in 1932. He studied philosophy at the Gregorian University in Rome, Italy. Priest. Back in Spain on a break from school, Father Xavier was imprisoned for several days and then executed in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War for the crime of being a priest. Martyr.
