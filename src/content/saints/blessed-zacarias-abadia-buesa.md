Educated by the Salesians from age 9, when he was old enough he joined the Society, making his profession in 1930. He trained at the Salesian Institute of Sarriá near Barcelona, Spain, and taught there. He wanted to leave for the missions, but the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War caused him to go into hiding with family, and then on his own. He was eventually found by the militia and executed. Martyr.
