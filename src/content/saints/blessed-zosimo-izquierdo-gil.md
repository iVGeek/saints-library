Ordained a priest in the diocese of Zaragoza, Spain on 20 March 1920. Member of the Dominican Fraternity of Priests. Parish priest for 16 years, concerned with the spiritual lives of his people, caring nothing for politics; but the political turmoil came for him. Martyred in the Spanish Civil War for the crime of being a priest.
