One of the devotional titles for the Blessed Virgin Mary. It is based on a letter that the Christians in Messina, Sicily received from Mary, commending them for their faith and assuring them of her protection. The letter was tied up with a lock of her hair, which is still enshrined as a relic in the cathedral of Messina.
