Son of Christopher who had been prime minister to Pope John VIII. Cardinal–priest of Saint Susanna. 123rd pope. Little is known of his seven month reign. Issued a Bull ordering the bishops of Dalmatia to obey him and confine their operations within limits of their own dioceses.
