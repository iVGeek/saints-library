Born a princess in 13th-century Bohemia, the daughter of Premislaus Ottocar I, king of Bohemia and Abdela or Adela, daughter of Otto, margrave of Meissen. Half-sister of Saint Agnes of Bohemia. Nun. Abbess of Gerenrhoda Abbey.
