Daughter of Saint Vincent Madelgarus and Saint Waldetrudis; sister of Saint Madalberta; niece of Saint Aldegund of Maubeuge; grand-daughter of Saint Walbert and Saint Bertilla. Nun and then, in 660 abbess at the convent led by her aunt Aldegund.
