Born to the Hungarian nobility. In the late 5th century, with the support of Bishop Ruricius I, he became a hermit at the conflux of the Glanne and Vienne Rivers near Comodoliac (modern Saint-Junien), diocese of Limoges, France. Spiritual teacher of Saint Junian. Reported miracle worker.
