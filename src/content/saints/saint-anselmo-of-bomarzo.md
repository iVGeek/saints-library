Born to a wealthy, pious family who encouraged Anselmo from an early age to study the Bible. When the last of his family died, the young man gave away his wealth to the poor, and retired to live as a studious hermit. However, as he was the most learned and pious man they knew, he was soon chosen by the local parishioners and clergy to serve as the new bishop of Bomarzo, Italy. He was known as a miracle worker who, through prayer, healed the sick, drove demons out of the possessed, and caused miraculous weather to drive away would-be invaders from the city.
