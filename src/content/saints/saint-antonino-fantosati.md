Antonino joined the Franciscan Order of Reformed Friars Minor on 28 July 1862 at the convent of Spineta in Todi, Italy. Ordained a priest on 13 June 1865. Missionary to China in 1867; he served for his remaining 33 years. Chosen vicar apostolic for southern Hunan, China and titular bishop of Adraa on 5 April 1892. One of the Martyrs of China, killed during the Boxer Rebellion.
