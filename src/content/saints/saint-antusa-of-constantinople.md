Born a princess, the daughter of the inconoclastic Emperor Konstantinos Kopronimos and Empress Irene; her twin brother became emperor Leo IV. Antusa was named for Saint Anthusa of Constantinople who had predicted the empress's pregnancy, and that she was carrying twins. The empress died when Antusa was very young. Early feeling a call to religious life, she wore a hairshirt under her imperial gowns. Around the year 775, upon her father's death, Antusa answered her call and became a nun, receiving the habit from Bishop Tarasio. She dedicated herself, her influence and her wealth to helping the poor, freeing slaves, repairing churches, adopting orphans and building monasteries. In her own convent she did the most menial of chores, and would never to sit to eat at meal time but instead waited on her sisters. When her brother died in 780, Antusa was invited to join the imperial government, but she preferred and stayed as a nun.
