Monk and missionary to Sicily, working in the area of Sutera, Italy. He spent his later days as a prayerful ascetic cave hermit near the town of Paternò, Italy.
The records of this saint apparently compress several people of this name with similar works, because stories variously say –
&bull; he was a spiritual student of Saint Peter the Apostle, which would place him in the 1st or 2nd century;
&bull; he worked with Saint Philip of Agrira, whose own times are a little confused, and is place in either the 1st or 5th centuries;
&bull; he worked with Saint Calogero of Sicily, which would be in the early to mid 6th century;
&bull; he knew Saint Onophrius, which indicates the late 4th century.
