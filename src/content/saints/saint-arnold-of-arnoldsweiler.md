Harpist and singer at the court of Blessed Charlemagne. Pilgrim to Santiago de Compostela in Spain.
Part of the tradition of devotion to Saint Arnold is a donation of candles, sometimes referred to as “wax interest” or “wax sacrifice”. For several centuries, parishes and villages in the area with a connection to Saint Arnold would, on Pentecost Tuesday, make donations of one-half to twelve pounds of candles at each altar for use by the churches.
