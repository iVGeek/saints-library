Monk at the Menat monastery in Auvergne, France. Abbot of the Micy monastery near Orléans, France. Hermit in area of Perche, France. His reputation for holiness led to would-be spiritual students to gather around his shack. Eventually there were so many that they were led to build a new monastery where Avitus served as abbot.
