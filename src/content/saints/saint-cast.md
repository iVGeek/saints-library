Monk in 6th-century Ireland and Wales. Spiritual student of Saint Jacut. He travelled from Wales to Armorica, an area of France now known as Brittany; legend says that once, when he wanted to consult with Saint Jacut, the sea split and allowed him to walk back to the monastery. Pilgrim to Rome, Italy, where he may have died, possible as a martyr; records are unclear.
