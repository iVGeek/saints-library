Spiritual student of Saint Amandus of Belgium. Benedictine monk at the monastery of Elnone (modern Saint-Amand-les-Eaux) in Tournai, Flanders (in modern Belgium). Provost (administrative assistant to the abbot) of the abbey of Marchiennes near Douai, France. Legend says that Crodobald was once struck with a painful illness for having disobeyed Saint Amandus; he was miraculously cured when he did penance and showed true contrition.
