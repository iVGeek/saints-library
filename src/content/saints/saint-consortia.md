Born into the nobility, daughter of Saint Eucherius of Lyon, and Galla; sister of Saint Veranus of Vence, Saint Salonius of Geneva, and Saint Tullia. Miraculously healed the dying daughter of King Clotaire. In reward, the king endowed a convent in an area of modern France, and Consotia fled there to escape a series of marriage proposals and life the religious life. Venerated at Cluny, France.
