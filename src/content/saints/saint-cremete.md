Hermit in an ruined hermitage between Mount Etna and the Placa Forest during a period when Sicily was under Saracen domination. When the island returned to Christian control in the late 11th century, Cremete got support from Prince Ruggero I to rebuild and re-staff a ruined monastery. Beginning in 1092, Cremete then lived as a Basilian monk and later abbot of what became San Salvatore di Placa in Francavilla di Sicilia, Messina, Sicily, Italy.
