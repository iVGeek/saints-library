Early, possibly the first, bishop of the diocese of Vaison, France; he is documented as having attended Council of Arles in 314, so was serving by then, and continued in the office until 347. Local legend says that he was sent to evangelize the area by the Apostles, but that seems to be wrong by a couple of centuries.
