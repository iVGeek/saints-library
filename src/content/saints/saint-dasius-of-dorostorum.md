Bishop at Dorostorum (modern Silistra, Bulgaria). Fought against the immorality involved in the Saturnalia and other pagan festivals. Martyred in the persecutions of Diocletian.
Some stories say that he was not a bishop but a Christian soldier who kept his faith to himself as a way to avoid trouble. At some point in the 4th century he was chosen king of the Saturnalia; this would have put him in the middle of the pagan celebrations, and would have ended in his death as a sacrifice. Rather than live and die in such a fashion, he publicly proclaimed himself a Christian. He was imprisoned, ordered to make sacrifice to the gods, refused, and was executed.
