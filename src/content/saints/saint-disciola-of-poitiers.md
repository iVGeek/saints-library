Niece of Saint Salvius, bishop of Albi, France. Disciola became a nun at the abbey of Santa Croce in Poitiers, France in the 6th century where she was a spiritual student of Saint Agnes of Poitiers. Disciola became known for her humility, her personal piety, and her dedication of the Rule of her house.
