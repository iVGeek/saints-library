Seventh-century parish priest in the diocese of Auxerre, France, serving under Saint Vigilius of Auxerre. He revitalized the faith in his parish with his personal holiness and his preaching. Modern Saint-Andelain, France grew up on the land, a gift from Saint where Domnolus used to retreat for solitary prayer.
