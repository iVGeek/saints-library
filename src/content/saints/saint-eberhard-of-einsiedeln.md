Provost of the cathedral of Strasbourg (on the modern France and German border. In 934 he retired to live as a hermit in the Black Forest for several years. In the mid-940's, he and several other area hermits joined together as a Benedictine house. On 27 October 947, King Otto I confirmed the founding of the Einsiedeln Abbey in Switzerland. Everard then served the rest of his life as its first abbot.
