Son of King Edgar the Peaceful, and &AElig;thelfl&aelig;d. On Edgar's death in 975, there was a disputed succession between Edward and his younger half-brother, &AElig;thelred, Edgar's son by &AElig;lfthryth, but Edward was chosen King of England at age 13; he reigned less than three years. Killed at the behest of his step-mother Elfrida so her son could take the throne, and popularly proclaimed a martyr.
