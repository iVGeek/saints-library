Giuseppe joined the Franciscan Friars Minor (Observants) at the age of 18, taking the name Elia. Ordained a priest in 1864. Missionary to China, leaving Marseille, France in October 1867. There he worked to train Chinese seminarians. Martyred in the Boxer Rebellion.
