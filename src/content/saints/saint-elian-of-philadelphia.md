Elian was a weaver and zealous Christian in the city of Philadelphia (modern Amman, Jordan). During a period of food shortages, the city was rocked by disturbances against the civil authorities. The civic leaders were imprisoned by the provincial governor for letting things get out of hand. Elian managed to sneak into the prison to bring them aid, and when they asked why he had done it, he explained the Gospel to them. When the prisoners were later ordered to make sacrifices to a pagan idol as a start of their return to society, they refused; they were whipped and released. When the governor learned that their behavior was due to Elian's ministry, he had the weaver imprisoned, tortured and executed. Martyr.
