Born to the North African nobility, both Elpidius and his brother Canione became priests. Tortured and exiled by being stuck in a boat without a rudder and sent out to sea in the persecutions of the Arian Vandals led by Genseric. The boat landed in the Campania region of southern Italy, and he resumed his ministry with the people there. Bishop of Atella where he served for 22 years. One of the Exiles of Campania.
