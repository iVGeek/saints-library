Born to the Saxon nobility; the sister of Bishop Meinwerk of Paderborn, Germany. Lay woman, married to Count Luidger, the son of a Saxon duke. In 1008 she built a church on land given to her by the pope. Mother of one, who would become Bishop Imad of Paderborn. Widowed in 1011, she received many offers of marriage, and much pressure from her family to re-marry, but instead she devoted herself and her fortune to charity to the poor of Bremen, Germany, support of the cathedral, and the construction of churches.
