Born to the wealthy nobility, the daughter of Ermeonoldo and Armensinda, and related to the Pepin family. Ermelinda was early drawn to religious life, and when her family arranged a marriage for her, she refused, and left home to seek prayer and solitude. She lived as a hermitess near Beauvechain, Belgium, spending her days alone, her nights in prayer in a local church. Two local lords discovered her identity, and planned to carry her away in hopes of marriage, but Ermelinda fled the area to escape them; tradition says she was warned of the plan by an angel. She became an anchoress in Meldaert, Belgium where she lived the rest of her days.
