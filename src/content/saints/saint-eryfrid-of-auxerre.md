Born to the Breton nobility, the son of Erifrifo and Iseberga. Priest. Chosen bishop of Auxerre, France by King Charles the Simple, he was consecrated on 29 August 887. He rebuilt the cathedral which had been destroyed by fire, was known for his personal kindness, and care for the poor. Legend says that he was miraculously cured of gout by a piece of metal brought him by a crow. Late in life he developed a paralysis that kept him bed-ridden.
