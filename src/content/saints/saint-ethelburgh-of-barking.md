Related to the East Anglian royal family and sister of Saint Erconwald of London. Nun and a spiritual student of Saint Hildelith of Barking. First abbess of Barking Abbey in Essex, England. Founded the church of All Hallows Berkyngechirche (All Hallows Barking; All Hallows by the Tower) in London, England.
