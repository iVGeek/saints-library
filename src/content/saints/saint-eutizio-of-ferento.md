Young priest who, after celebrating Mass in honor of the recently martyred Saint Gracilian and Saint Felicissima, was arrested by imperial soldiers, tortured and executed in the persecutions of Aurelian. Martyr.
Legend says that one day Eutizio was walking around Ferento and saw a farmer plowing a field with a team of oxen. The oxen, tired from work and weak from hunger, stopped pulling the plow, angering the farmer. Father Eutizio went into the field, prayed, and threw some seeds on the ground. The seeds immediately sprouted, grew into a bunch of grain, the oxen ate it, and returned to tilling the field.
