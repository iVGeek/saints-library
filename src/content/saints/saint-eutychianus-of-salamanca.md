Brother of Saint Paschasius of Salamanca and Saint Paulillus of Salamanca. Courtier who accompanied the Vandal King Genseric to Africa. When anti-Christian persecutions began, he was first banished from court, then, when he continue to use the Nicene Creed, was imprisoned, tortured and martyred.
