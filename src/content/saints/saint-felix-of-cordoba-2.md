Layman in Moorish controlled ninth-century Spain. Married to Saint Liliosa of Cordoba. A covert Christian who was careful not to display enough of his faith to risk the attention of Muslim neighbors. However, stories of the persecutions of active Christians shamed him into openly living his faith. Martyred in the persecutions of Caliph Abderraham II.
