The son of Anselmo Croese and Maria Antonia Garza, Giovanni was baptized at the age of two days. He joined the Order of Friars Minor Capuchin on 14 October 1822, taking the name Francis Mary, and passing his novitiate at the convent of Saint Barnabas outside Genoa, Italy. Known for his works of charity, working in the street and on the docks with the poor. He died of cholera caught while caring for the sick during a cholera epidemic.
