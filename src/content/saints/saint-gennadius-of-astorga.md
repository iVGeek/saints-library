Benedictine monk at Argeo, Spain. Abbot of San Pedro de Montes, which he and 12 brother monks, including Saint Urban of Penalba, restored in 895. Under the guidance of abbot Gennadius, the house helped spread the Benedictine Rule through northwest Spain. Bishop of Astorga, Spain for 35 years. Resigned his see c.931, and retired to live his remaining years as a monk and hermit at San Pedro.
