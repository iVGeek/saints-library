Grew up a shepherd on the island of Crete. He lived as a prayerful pilgrim beggar in Jerusalem and the Holy Lands for 12 years where he was regularly abused for his faith by the area Muslims. He then made a pilgrimage to the tombs of the Apostles in Rome, Italy, and stayed to enter monastic life. He became a friend of Saint Michael the Confessor when Michael was ambassador to the Vatican, and Gregory accompanied him back to Constantinople. He spent his remaining days as a monk at the monastery on the cape of Akritas where he was known for extended penances and a severely ascetic life.
