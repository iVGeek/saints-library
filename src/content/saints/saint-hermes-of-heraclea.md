Cleric at Heraclea near Constantinople. Arrested and abused with other Christians by the authorities who demanded the location of sacred texts so they could be burned. The group refused to say where they had hidden their copies of the Scriptures, and were executed. Martyr A copy of the court proceedings against the group has survived to today.
