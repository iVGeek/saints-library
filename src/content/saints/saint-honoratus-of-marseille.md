Spiritual student of Saint Honoratus of Arles; he wrote a biography of Honoratus. Bishop of Marseille (in modern France) in the latter 5th century where he was known as a capable preacher and speaker. He carried on a lengthy correspondence with Pope Saint Gelasius I where they discussed orthodox Christianity in a time of multiple heresies, but these writings have been lost.
