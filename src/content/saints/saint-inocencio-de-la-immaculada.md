Born to a farming family, Manuel early felt a call to religious life, and joined the Passionist as a young man, making his profession on 27 July 1905. Ordained a priest in Ovieda, Spain on 20 September 1913. He taught literature, philosophy and theology, and brought his teaching skills into the pulpit where he became a preacher known to his clear, intense sermons. He was transferred to the convent of Mieries in the Asturias region of northern Spain in September 1934. One of the Martyrs of Astoria killed in the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War.
