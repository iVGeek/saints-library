Hermit at Réome, France. His reputation for holiness spread, and he began to attract would-be disciples. To escape them he sneaked away, and became a monk at Lérins Abbey. He was sent by his bishop back to Réome as abbot of its monastery, which became Moutiers-Saint-Jean in his honour. Spiritual teacher of Saint Sylvester of Réome. He became one of the pioneers of Western monasticism. So strict in his refusal to be around women, he refused to receive his own mother when she visited the monastery.
