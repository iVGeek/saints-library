Father of Saint Theodorus of Apamea and Saint Theophanes of Nicaea. When his sons were grown and entering religious life, Jonah became a monk at the Grand Laura of San Saba in the Kidron Valley in Palestine for the rest of his life. There he was known as a model of asceticism and holiness.
