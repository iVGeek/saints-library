Daughter of Yak Jong Church who was martyred in 1801 in the persecution of Shin-Yu, an attack on the faith that killed all the clergy in the country. Daughter of Saint Cecilia Yu Sosa; sister of Saint Paul Chong Ha-Sang. One of the Martyrs of Korea.
