Born to the Gallo-Roman nobility, he was raised in a pious Christian family, and dedicated to God from an early age. As a young man he lived as a hermit in Bois Trappeau in Ardilleux, France. His reputation for holiness spread, he attracted would-be spiritual students, and in 559 founded the Benedictine monastery at Mairé (Mariacum) in Mairé-Levescault, France. Friend of Saint Radegunde. Later in life he retired to live as a hermit in Chaulny, France. The monastery survived for 1,000 years, but was destroyed in the French Revolution.
