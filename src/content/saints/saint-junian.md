The son of the Count of Cambrai, Junian felt a call to religion; at age 15, in the late 5th century, became a hermit and spiritual student of Saint Amandus of Limoges at Commodoliacus (modern Saint-Junien, France). He lived at the site for about 40 years, and was known as a miracle worker.
