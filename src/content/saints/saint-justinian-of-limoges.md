Justinian's parents, Aniano and Giusta, were a childless pagan couple converted to Christianity by Saint Martial of Limoges. When they brought up the lack of children, Martial said that they should pray for a child. Soon after, Giusta became pregnant, and when Justinian was born, the parents took him to Martial to be baptized. The little guy died at the age of four days as his parents were taking him home. Aniano and Giusta, and some other local new converts, believed that the boy must have gone to heaven, and began praying for his intercession; they began to receive miraculous help, which increased their devotion and solidified their faith.
