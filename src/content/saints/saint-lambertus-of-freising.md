Bishop of Freising, Bavaria, Germany from 955 to 957, serving during a period of political turmoil and invasion by Hungarians.
With little documentation about his life, legends became attached to him, such as performing holy fasts while still an infant, or his ability to cloak the cathedral of Freising in fog in 937, and thus render is invisible and safe from the invading Hungarians.
