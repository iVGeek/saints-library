The son of Albino della Croce and Sabina Nicodemo. Monk of Saint Peter's Abbey near Aquara, Italy, joining at the age of 15. Following his training, he served at Saint Peter's, at the Monte Cassino Abbey and in Salerno, Italy. Counselor to Prince Guaimaro in Salerno. Pilgrim to the Holy Lands. Founded the monastery of Santa Maria dell Albabeta near Montecassino, and served as its prior.
