Brother of Saint Luglianus. Regional bishop in Ireland. In 696 the brothers joined a party making a pilgrimage to the Holy Land. When their ship got stranded in Boulogne-sur-Mer, France, the two began preaching the faith through the region, including the diocese of Thérouanne. Martyr. Many legends became attached to them both of their former lives in Ireland, and the miracles that resulted into their intercession.
