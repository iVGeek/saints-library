Traditionally a wanderer from an unspecified distant country, Macarius lived for many years as a hermit in a chapel in Valdesaz, Guadalajara, diocese of Toledo, Spain, known for his piety, simple life, and teaching of the faith. He apparently dressed all in black, which has led some writers to guess that he was a Benedictine, and then to invent back stories about him which lead to him sometimes being depicted as a mitred abbot or other clerical garb.
