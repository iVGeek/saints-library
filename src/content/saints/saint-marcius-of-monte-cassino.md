Born to the Italian nobility. Hermit on Monte Cassino when Saint Benedict arrived to start his abbey; the two fasted and prayed together about the proper religious life, and when they came to different conclusions, Marcius became a hermit on Mount Massicus; he spent several years sealed in a narrow cave there as a way to increase his solitude and penance. Pope Saint Gregory the Great wrote about him. Known as a miracle worker.
