The daughter of Jose Molas and Maria Vallvé, Rosa was baptized at the age of one day. She joined the Daughters of Charity in 1841, taking the name Maria Rosa, worked in and then directed a girls' sewing school, served as superior, and worked in a hospital. With the help and support of her bishop, and for reasons that apparently had more to do with political differences than anything else, she left the Daughters and on 14 March 1857 she formed the women's congregation of theSisters of Our Lady of Consolation dedicated to the poor, to teaching, ministering to the sick, and supporting the faithful in hospitals and colleges. They continue their good work today in eleven countries across four continents.
