Teenaged lay woman in the apostolic vicariate of Southeastern Zhili, China. Daughter of Saint Maria Zhao Guoshi; sister of Saint Rosa Zhao. During the persecutions of the Boxer Rebellion, the three of them hid in a well to avoid being raped. They were found, dragged out, and killed for being Christian. Martyrs.
