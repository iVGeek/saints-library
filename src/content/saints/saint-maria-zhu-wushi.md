Married lay woman in the apostolic vicariate of Southeastern Zhili, China. Worked to support the missionary work of Saint Léon-Ignace Mangin and Saint Paul Denn and martyred with them in the Boxer Rebellion. She was killed while trying to protect Saint Léon who was distributing Holy Communion.
