Monk at Bobbio, Italy. He founded Precipiano Abbey near Tortona, Italy, having been assigned there by Saint Attalas.
Legend says that at one point Maroveus set fire to a grove of trees sacred to local pagans; they caught him, beat him and, thinking he was dead, threw him into a local river. He survived, his attackers were stricken with a serious illness, and if they repented their attack, were healed by the prayers of Maroveus.
