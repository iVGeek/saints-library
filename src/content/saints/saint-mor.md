Some old documents claim Mor was a boy resurrected from the dead by the prayers of Saint David of Wales, and that in gratitude, Mor carried David's copy of the Bible and served as a lectern, holding the Bible open for David to read to the people he evangelized. But there is no early evidence for any of this, and we know nothing definitive about him.
