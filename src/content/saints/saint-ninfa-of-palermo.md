The daughter of Aureliano, imperial Roman prefect of Palermo, Ninfa was raised in a pagan household. She was brought to Christianity by hearing the preaching of Saint Mamillian of Palermo, and was baptized by him. She and other Christians were imprisoned for their faith by the authorities, escaped (legend says freed by an angel who provided them a boat that sailed on its own), and fled from island to island along the coast of Italy before settling off the coast of Tuscany. Pilgrim to Rome, Italy, specifically to the tombs of Saint Peter and Saint Paul. No record about her death has survived, but the location of her relics indicate she died a martyr.
