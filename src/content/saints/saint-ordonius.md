Notary to the court of King Ferdinando I of Léon. Travelled with the king to combat Muslim forces. Benedictine monk in Sahagun, Léon (in modern Spain). Bishop of Astorga, Spain on 4 February 1062. Part of the group that brought the relics of Saint Justina and Saint Rufina to Léon.
