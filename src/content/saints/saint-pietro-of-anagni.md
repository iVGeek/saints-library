Born to the nobility, part of the family of the princes of Lombardy (part of modern Italy). Benedictine monk at the San Benito monastery in Salerno, Naples (in modern Italy). Priest. Papal chaplain. With the support of the future Pope Saint Gregory VII, Pietro was chosen bishop of Anagni, Latium (in modern Italy) by Pope Alexander II in 1062. He served there for 43 years, reformed and revitalized the clergy, and oversaw the finish of construction of the cathedral church. Friend, supporter and co-worker with Saint Bruno of Segni.  In 1071, Bishop Pietro became papal representative to Byzantine emperor Michael VII Ducas; when Michael fell ill, Pietro miraculously healed him. He later served in the First Crusade, being there at the fall of Jerusalem.
