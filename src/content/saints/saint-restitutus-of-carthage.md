Bishop of Carthage in North Africa from 352 to 373. Part of the Council of Rimini in 359 where he supported the Council of Nicaea. After some back and forth over the theology, he came down forcefully against Arianism. Saint Augustine of Hippo composed a sermon in honor of Restitutus.
