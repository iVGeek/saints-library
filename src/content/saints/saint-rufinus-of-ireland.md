The scraps of information we have about indicate he may have been a monk  or a hermit at the monastery of Glendalough, County Wicklow, Ireland, or the bishop of Glendalough, and/or of Bangor in County Down, Ireland, or he may have been something else entirely. All we know is that he appeared in multiple ancient martyrologies and thus there was a devotion to him in Ireland.
