Born to the Gallo-Roman nobility, the son of Salvius and Herchenfreda; brother of Saint Desiderius of Cahors. Rusticus early felt a called to religious life. Educated in the court of King Clotaire II. Archdeacon of the diocese of Rodez, France. Priest. Chaplain of the Palatine oratory. Bishop of Cahors, France in 623. Participated in the Council of Clichy in 627. Martyr.
