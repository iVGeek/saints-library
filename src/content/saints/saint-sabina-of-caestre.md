Born a princess, the daughter of King Genolfo of Mercia (in modern England); sister of Saint Elfrida and Saint Edith. Converting to Christianity, she renounced the marriage that her father had arranged, and with her sisters, left for a pilgrimage to Rome, Italy. After spending the night with some religious sisters in Cassel, Flanders, the three sisters were murdered by men hired by the would-be husbands they had turned down. Because the deaths were the direct result of their conversion, the sisters are considered martyrs.
