The son of King Owain Danwyn of Rhos; brother of King Cynlas of Rhos and King Saint Einion of Llyn. Called to religious life, Seiriol built himself a cell at the Penmon Priory in on the Isle of Anglesy; his brothers didn't think much of it, and rebuilt it. Friend of Saint Cybri; the two would weekly use a trip to the well to meet and catch up. Spiritual teacher of Saint Elaeth the King. Later in life he moved to live as a hermit on nearby Ynys Lannog (Puffin Island), which became known as Ynys Seiriol in his honour.
