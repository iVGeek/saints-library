Born a princess, the daughter of Count Even of Brest (in modern western France). She married the Count of Goëlo, an area of northern Brittany, and was the mother of Saint Budoc. Legend says she gave birth while locked in a barrel floating in the English channel, midwifed by an angel, having been exiled by her scheming stepmother. She and the baby landed near Beauport Abbey near Waterford, Ireland. They lived near the abbey, Sennara supporting by serving a laundress for the abbey; she developed a reputation for piety, and Budoc developed a vocation to religious life. A church in Zennor in west Cornwall is dedicated to Sennara, and there is a holy well with a tradition of healing women with insufficient milk.
