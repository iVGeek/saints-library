Born to the nobility of Aquitaine (in modern France). Married in her teens, whe was widowed at age 22. Though she was pressured to re-marry, she instead devoted her life to prayer and service to the poor. She founded the convent of Troclar in Lagrave (in modern southern France), entered as a sister, and served as its first abbess.
