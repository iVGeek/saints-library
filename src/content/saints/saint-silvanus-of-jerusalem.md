One of the original disciples, Silas is mentioned in the Acts of the Apostles as one of the leaders of the Church in Jerusalem, and in the letters of Saint Peter and Saint Paul as assisting in their missions. He became a travelling evangelist with Saint Paul and Saint Barnabas. He preached the Gospel in Antioch where he was considered a prophet. Helped Saint Peter with his writings. Martyr.
