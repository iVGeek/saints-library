Monk. Second abbot of Réome Abbey (modern Moutiers-Saint-Jean Abbey) near Dijon, France, selected by the founder of the house, Saint Jean of Réome; he served from 525 until his death eleven years later. Sylvester was known for his personal piety and for his support from King Clotaire and King Childebert.
