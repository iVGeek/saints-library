Eunuch. Member of the imperial Byzantine court. May have been the general of the Bucellarian division of the imperial army. Considered the leader of the 42 Martyrs of Amorium who were captured by invading Muslims, imprisoned for seven years, and then murdered when they refused to convert to Islam. Martyr.
