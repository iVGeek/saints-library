Eighth century consecrated virgin who worked with Saint Regulus in Scotland. Legend says that she was pursued by a young nobleman who raved about the beauty of her eyes; she went into hiding, plucked out her eyes, and sent them back to the noble, impaled on a pin to show how much she wanted to live the religious life. Her shrine was a centre of devotion and pilgrimage until destroyed in 1560 during the Scottish Reformation.
