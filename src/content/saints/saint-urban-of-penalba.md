Benedictine monk at Argeo, Spain in the late 9th century. In 895, Urban and a dozen other monks restored the abandoned San Pedro de Montes monastery in Bierzo, Spain. Spiritual student of Saint Gennadius of Astorga who served as his abbot. Founding abbot of Peñalba Abbey near Astorga, Spain c.912; he served for over 25 years.
