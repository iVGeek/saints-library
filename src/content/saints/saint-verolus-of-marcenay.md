Born to the aristocracy; his family was close to the Burgundian royals. Priest. Known as a miracle worker, he gave his wealth, work and miracles for the physical and spiritual improvement of the poor.
One day during Mass, Father Verolus fell into a trance and stood motionless at the altar for more than an hour. When he came back to himself, he said that he had being given a vision of a structure fire in the town of Plaines, about 15 miles away; there was a child sleeping in the building. While in the trance, Verolus went to the town and rescued the child. King Guntram, who was in the pews at the Mass, immediately ordered messengers to investigate, and after travelling to Plaines, they confirmed the story.
