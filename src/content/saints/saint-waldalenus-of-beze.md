Born to the wealthy Burgundian nobility, the son of Duke Almagar and Duchess Aquilina; he was the brother of Saint Adalsindis of Bèze, and was named after his grandfather. Educated by and spiritual student of Saint Columbanus. Monk of Luxeuil Abbey during the abbacies of Columbanus, Saint Eustace, and Saint Waldebert. Waldalenus was then assigned to found the abbey of Saints Peter and Paul in Bèze, France c.630, and then to serve as its first abbot. When a women's house was built nearby, Saint Adalsindis served as its first abbess. Under his leadership, the abbey expanded, and in 655 established a monastic school. Waldalenus served as a diplomat, and part of the court of Theoderic III. By the time of his death, the abbot had served the Bèze house for approximately 50 years.
