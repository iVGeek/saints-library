Raised in a poor family, William was born with crippled legs and missing his right hand. Priest. Pastor to the brothers of the monastery of Olux on the Durance River; he later served there as a canon regular. Legend says that he received three visits from an angel; the angel ordered him to warn the prior of a coming flood of the Durance that would drown the monastery. As a proof of the source of this information, the angel healed William of his infirmities. The prior was convinced, the priory was abandoned, and the brothers founded a new monastery. William was later chosen superior of the priory of Our Lady of Chalmes.
