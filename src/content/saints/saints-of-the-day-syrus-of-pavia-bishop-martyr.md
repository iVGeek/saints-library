1st century (?). Alleged first bishop of Pavia and said to have been sent there by the apostles. He probably belongs to the 3rd or 4th century. He is the principal patron of Pavia, Italy. See also under Juventius (Benedictines). You'll recognize Saint Syrus in art as a bishop enthroned between two deacons; sometimes with Saint Juventius (Roeder).
