A group of thousands of Christians martyred on Mount Ararat. Some tellings make them all soldiers, some that they were all imperial Roman soldiers, and some that they were led by Saint Acacius. The dates and the name of the persecutors vary, and modern scholarship points to this entry in old martyrologies being the result of combining the stories of many martyrdoms, and then legend piling on the story during re-tellings.
