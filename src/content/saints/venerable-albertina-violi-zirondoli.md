Born to a pious middle class family, Albertina earned a master's degree from a teacher's college in Modena, Italy in 1918 when she was only 17. On 31 May 1924, she married Livio Zirondoli whose family was firm in their socialist and materialist ideologies; the couple's son, Alfredo, grew up to be a priest.
Albertina spent a lifetime working as a teacher and forwarding the cause of education, and caring for the educational and spiritual lives of children. She organized non-school activities for children, on-going education for teachers, and ways to make continous learning a part of everyone's life. She became involved with the Focolare Movement, was a friend of Chiara Lubich, brought many to the organization, and its sprirituality informed her life and work.
