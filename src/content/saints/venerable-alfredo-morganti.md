A professed priest of the Order of Friars Minor in the province of Ancona, Italy, he taught philosophy, and served as spiritual director of young people just starting in religious life. Minister Provincial of the Order from 1936 to 1942. Visitator for his Order in various provinces of Italy. He taught at the Apostolic College of Saint Francis a Ripa in Rome, Italy from 1946 to 1957, and then served as spiritual director of the International College of Saint Anthony. Noted for attention to the Franciscan Rule both for himself, and those who he served as spiritual teacher. Late in life Father Berta returned to Matelica in Ancona where he served as confessor and spiritual director of Franciscan students.
