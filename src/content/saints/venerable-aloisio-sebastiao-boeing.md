Aloísio was raised in a pious family – his father João served as choirmaster and participated in Mass every day; his mother Josefina Effting had a devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary and Saint Joseph, and cared for travellers; of his six blood brothers and two adopted ones, two became priests, one a monk. Beginning in 1925, at age 11, Aloísio studied in the Dehonian seminary in Brusque, Brazil. He made his solemn profession in the Dehonians on 16 January 1938. Ordained a priest in Taubaté, Brazil on 1 December 1940. He served as parish priest in Varginha, Brazil, and professor in the seminary in Corupá, Brazil. Founded the Marian Fraternity of the Heart of Jesus in 1974.
