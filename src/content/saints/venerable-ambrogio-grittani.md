Priest in the diocese of Bari, Italy. Taught in the regional seminary in Molfetta, Italy. Founder of the Pious Work of Saint Benedict Joseph Labre in October 1941 with a mission to help the poor both materially and spiritually. Founder of the Oblate Sisters of Saint Benedict Joseph Labre. Founded the House of the Poor in Molfetta in 1949. Father Ambrogio lived poorer than the people he served, begged in the streets for people in need, and cared for the sick and the dying.
