Orphaned in her early teens, Amelia grew up in her uncle's house. She joined the Missionary Sisters of the Sacred Hearts of Jesus and Mary in Pula, Italy in 1906, taking the name Leonilde of Saint John the Baptist. After completing her studies, she began service as a teacher, and made her solemn profession on 2 August 1919. She served as supervisor the of the kindergarten school, was chosen Councilor General of her Congregation in 1926, and then Superior General in 1932, a position in which she served the rest of her life.
