Born to a wealthy family, Maria earned her teaching certification but had to return home for many years to care for her seriously ill mother. In 1888, following her mother's death, Maria tried to join the Daughters of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, but in 1889, because of her teaching background, she joined the Ursuline Institute of Somasca. On 5 September 1893, in order to establish a new boarding school and orphanage in Gazzuolo, Italy, Mother Margherita and six fellow Ursulines made the move. She co-founded the Congregation of the Ursuline Sisters of the Sacred Heart of Jesus of Asola, was chosen Vicar-General of the Sisters in 1894, and Superior-General in 1924. She was known for her charity, her practical and common-sense approach to problem solving, and her dedication to the Eucharist and the Sacred Heart of Jesus.
