Daughter of the vice-consul of Italy in Brazil, she grew up watching her family fortunes dwindle following the death of her father and financial irregularities by her uncles. Seeing her lack of position in the world as a way to follow a call to religion, Maria joined the Passionist Sisters of Saint Paul of the Cross, taking the name Sister Antoinetta of Saint Michael the Archangel. She held many offices and positions within the Sisters, serving as novice mistress, school director and Provincial Superior along with ministering to the sick, the dying and prisoners. The brain tumor that killed her ruined her health overall in the final months of her life, eventually leaving her blind and then paralyzed.
