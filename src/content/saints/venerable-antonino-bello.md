After studying in Bologna and Rome, Italy, Antonino was ordained a priest in the diocese of Ugento, Italy on 8 December 1957. Chosen bishop of Molfetta, Giovinazzo e Terlizzi, Italy on 10 August 1982. Chosen bishop of Ruvo, Italy on 30 September 1982 as it prepared to unite to form the diocese of Molfetta-Ruvo-Giovinazzo-Terlizzi, Italy. Member of the Secular Franciscan Order. Noted for his work as a pastor and for involving lay people to reinvigorate the diocese.
