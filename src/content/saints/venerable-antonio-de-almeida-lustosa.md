Antônio studied at the Salesian college of Cachoreira do Campo, Brazil, and joined the Order. Ordained a priest on 28 January 1912. Bishop of Uberaba, Brazil on 4 July 1924. Bishop of Corumbá, Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil on 17 December 1928. Archbishop of Belém do Pará, Brazil on 10 July 1931. Archbishop of Fortaleza, Ceara, Brazil on 19 July 1941 where he served for over 20 years. Titular Archbishop of Velebusdus on 16 February 1963. He worked in stained glass, including the windows in the cathedral of Fortaleza, and over the years wrote works on theology, philosophy, spirituality, hagiography, literature, geology, and botany. Father Antônio spent his last 15 years in retirement at the Salesian house of Carpina, praying and writing.
