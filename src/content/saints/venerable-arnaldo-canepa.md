Lifelong layman of the diocese of Rome, Italy. He was indifferent to religion during the first half of his life, having a conversion experience in May 1921. Served as a catechist. Founder of the Centro Oratori Romani in 1945. He devoted his life and impoverished himself in support the work of the Oratories to teach the faith.
