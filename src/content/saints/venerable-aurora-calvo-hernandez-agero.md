Raised in deeply religious family, Aurora was educated at the Collegio della Religiose della Sacra Famiglia di Béjar (College of Religious of the Holy Family of Béjar), and was close to her older brother Ramón, who became a Jesuit missionary priest in Cuba and Puerto Rico. Aurora was drawn to religious life, and wanted to become a Discalced Carmelite, but she needed to care for her aging mother, her father having died when the girl was four years old, and held off entering a contemplative order. Instead, she became more involved in service as a lay woman, working as a catechist, spending free time in spiritual exercises and devotions. At the recommendation or her spiritual director, she wrote a diary of her spiritual life and insights; it shows that she saw the spiritual life in everything, and cared for nothing but the approval of God.
