The 8th of nine children born to a peasant family, her heart was damaged at age 8 by rheumatic fever. This led to a life of alternating hospitalizations and recovery at home, but also to a life of intercessory prayer. Member of Catholic Action, of Unitalsi and of the Center for Volunteers of Suffering.
