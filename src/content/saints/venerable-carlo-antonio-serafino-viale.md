From his youth, Carlo felt drawn to religious life. He joined Franciscan Friars Minor at the convent of Oregina, Italy on 29 August 1847, taking the name Fra Giacomo. He studied at several convents in the area of Genoa, Italy, and on 17 December 1852 was ordained a priest. He served in convents in Genoa and Ventimiglia, Italy, and on 4 February 1863 became parish priest in Bordighera, Italy where he served for most of his remaining 49 years. Founded the House of Providence to help the poor.
