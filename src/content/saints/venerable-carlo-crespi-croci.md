The third of thirteen children born to Daniele Crespi and Luisa Croci. He studied with and joined the Salesians of Don Bosco, making his profession in 1910. He studied in Valsalice, Italy, and was ordained a priest in 1917.
Missionary to Cuenca, Ecuador in 1923. He founded four schools, a theater, an orphanage, a museum, and got electricity installed in a local village. In addition to his teaching and preaching, he was known as a botanist, microbiologist, musician, archaeologist, and devotee to Mary Help of Christians.
