Ordained a priest on 17 December 1814. Superior of the seminary at Cimiez, Nice, France in 1822. Entered the Missionary Oblates of Mary Immaculate at Aix-la-Chappelle in 1824; professed by Apostolic indult after a few months novitiate. Missionary to Corsica in 1835 where his work in the pulpit and the confessional won many souls.
