The eldest of 17 children born to Carlo Vigevano and Giuditta Colzi. Gaetano's father was a tailor, and the boy grew up in the trade, serving as an assistant. He was a pious child, devoted to the Eucharist, to Our Lady, to caring for the poor, and serving as youth leader in his parish. On 8 November 1852 he became a Capuchin novice at the Annunciation convent in Borno, Italy; he had to leave the Order for several months for health reasons, but make his profession on 30 March 1855, taking the name Carlo Maria da Abbiategrasso. He was ordained a priest on 26 December 1855, and continued his study of theology for three more years. He was transferred to the convent of Casalpusterlengo, Italy in 1858 where he was known to heal people by praying over them. Word spread, the convent became a pilgrimage point, and was flooded by mailed requests for prayers and blessings; the government asked that he be transferred to reduce the traffic and crowds, but the bishop refused.
