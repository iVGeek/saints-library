Young layperson of the diocese of Novara, Italy who suffered from a rare skin disease (dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa) that required frequent medical treatment including hospitalization and blood transfusions. She was a member of the Focolare Movement, a leader in the parish youth group, and as much of a normal teenager as she could be with her medical problems. She wrote and argued against abortion and euthanasia, and her piety was inspiring to all who knew her.
