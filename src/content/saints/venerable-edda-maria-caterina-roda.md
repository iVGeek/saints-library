One of three children born to Guerrino, a baker, and Cecilia Capelli; she made her First Communion on 18 April 1948 at the church of the Visitation in Bagnolo Mella, Italy, and was Confirmed there on 17 September 1950. Edda was studying in Bergamo, Italy to become a nurse when she came to know the Capuchin Sisters of Mother Rubatto at the San Francesco Clinic. Drawn to their mission and life, Edda joined in 1960, initially taking the name Adalbert of the Most Holy Trinity; she later changed to Edda of the Holy Trinity. She served in home missions and as a catechist. For several years she was repeatedly hospitalized with “asthenic syndrome” which leads to extreme physical weakness and depression. She continued her work whenever she was strong enough, and her ability to explain the Bible lead to an extensive correspondence with people who wished to understand it better.
