Studied theology at the Universidad Central de Madrid. Ordained in the diocese of Plasencia, Spain in April 1865. Parish priest. Taught in the seminary. Canon penitentiary. Spiritual director of the Augustinian Recollect Sisters of Serradilla. Founded the Josefinas de la Santísima Trinidad (Josephine Sisters of the Most Holy Trinity).
