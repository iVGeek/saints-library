Born to the nobility, the eldest of eight children born to Thomman and Thanda of Vyppissery. She was known as a pious and compassionate child with an early devotion to the Eucharist and desire for religious life. However, giving in to her family wishes, she married Vatharu Vakayil in 1847. They had a daughter, Anna, on 21 April 1950. Vatharu died of natural causes in late 1851. Eliswa was pressured to re-marry, but instead she prayed that the Holy Spirit would guide her to proper works as a pious widow.
Her example attracted her youngest sister, Thresia, and her own daughter to a contemplative prayer life. With them as the core, and with the aid of her spiritual director, Eliswa built a convent and founded the Congregation of the Third Order of Discalced Carmelite Sisters (now the Teresian Carmelites), the first regligious congregation for women in the Indian state of Kerala; she became the first religious sister in Kerala, and later established the first convent school, boarding home, and orphanage for girls in the state.
