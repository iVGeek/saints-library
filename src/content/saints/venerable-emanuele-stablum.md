Religious brother, joining the Sons of the Immaculate Conception on 15 August 1913. By order of his Congregation superiors, he gave up studying for the priesthood to study medicine, and graduated medical school in 1930. In 1943 and 1944, he used his position in the Istituto Dermopatico dell'Immacolata hospital in Rome, Italy to save hundreds of people who were refugees from the Nazis, including 52 Jews who were destined for the concentration camps; this led to him being declared “Righteous among the Nations” by Yad Vashem. Co-founder of the Italian Catholic Doctors Association in 1945.
