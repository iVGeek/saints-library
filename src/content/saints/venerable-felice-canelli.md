Born to a very poor family, Felice early felt a call to religion, entered the minor seminary at age 12 and was ordained a priest of the diocese of San Severo, Italy on 6 June 1903. In 1905 the Salesians of Don Bosco began working in his diocese; Father Felice was drawn to their charism, and worked with them and their mission to young people for the rest of his life. Appointed parish priest of Santa Croce in San Severo in 1927, he served as parish priest there for 50 years.
