Baptized at the age of one day. Educated by Capuchins, he joined them at the convent of Lovere, Italy on 23 June 1891, and made his solemn profession at the convent of Brescia, Italy on 2 July 1896. Missionary to Brazil, arriving on 30 August 1898. Ordained a priest in the cathedral of Fortaleza, Brazil on 19 March 1899. On 22 January 1900 he was assigned to the Colonia de S. Antonio do Prata leper colony in Parà, Brazil where he served for almost 14 years and contracted the disease himself. On 31 January 1913 he left Prata for Anil do Maranhão, then back to Prata, then the Retiro S. Francisco where he lived his remaining years in isolation.
