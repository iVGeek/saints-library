A member of the Order of Friars Minor Conventual, he joined on 23 April 1905 and made his solemn profession on 15 August 1909. Ordained a priest on 10 August 1912. Appointed prefect apostolic of Ndola, Zambia on 4 February 1938, vicar apostolic of Ndola on 13 Jauary 1949, and then bishop of Ndola when it was elevated to a diocese on 25 April 1959; he retired from the position on 26 November 1965. Titular bishop of Coeliana on 13 January 1949, and titular bishop of Casae Nigrae on 26 November 1965. He served as a Council Father at all four sessions of Vatican II.
