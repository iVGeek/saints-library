Ordained a priest in the diocese of Cosenza, Italy on 14 July 1912. He served as a parish priest in Montalto Uffugo where he turned an old convent into a larger center for youth ministry. He served as a military chaplain in Friuli, Italy during World War I, and spent a period in imprisoned in the Austrian concentration camp of Katzenau; there he contracted tuberculosis. He returned to the Calabria region of Italy in 1919, and in answer to what he saw as a distressing lack of catechesis and Christian life, founded the Pii operai catechisti rurali (Congregation of Pious Workers Rural Catechists; Ardorini Missionaries). Comprised of both priests and lay people, the Missionaries have a call of teaching about the faith, and how live the faith.
