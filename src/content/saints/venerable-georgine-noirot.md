Drawn to religious life from her youth, Georgine joined the Institute of the Daughters of Saint Vincent de Paul at age 18. She studied in Naples, Italy, and then assigned to Imola, Italy in 1852 to work as a teacher and minister to poor and orphaned girls. She made her profession in the Daughters on 25 May 1856.
Due to political events, the Daughters were recalled from Imola in 1873. Georgine felt compelled to continue to work with the girls there, and after much prayer, and with the approval of Bishop Luigi Tesorieri of Imola, she founded the Congregation of the Handmaids of the Sacred Heart of Jesus under the Protection of Saint Joseph, served as its first director, and with three sisters made her profession on 19 June 1879. They soon spread their good work through the region.
