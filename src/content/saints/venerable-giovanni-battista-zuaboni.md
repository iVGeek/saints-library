Orphaned at the age of two. Ordained a priest in the diocese of Brescia, Italy on 9 June 1906. Developed a ministry to soldiers recovering in a Brescia military hospital. Founded the organization that became the modern Institute for the Family. Founded the Secular Institute of the Company of the Holy Family.
