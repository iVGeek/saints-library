Born to the nobility, Giovanni studied law in Pavia, Italy, and was a friend of Saint Charles Borromeo. Ordained a priest on 29 July 1576. He joined the Barnabites in 1578, taking the name Carlo in honour of Saint Charles. He served in various administrative functions in the order. Chosen bishop of Novara, Italy on 8 February 1593 by Pope Clement VIII, bishop Carlo became a prominent civic leader, implemented reforms in religious life, and applied all the decrees and decisions of the Council of Trent; this latter led to multiple assassination attempts including trying to burn down the episcopal palace with him in it. He worked for the beatification of Saint Charles Borromeo, and was present at the canonization celebration. Wrote several ascetic, juridical, and historical works.
