Licensed as a banker, as a young adult Giovanni worked at the Credito Italiano and at the Cassa di Risparmio banks in Genoa, Italy. Feeling a call to religious life, in 1918 Giovanni joined the Order of Friars Minor Capuchin. Priest. He studied at Roman University, and worked on several publications. In the aftermath of World War II, he worked to care for orphans and abandoned children, and founded the Sorriso Francescano Foundation on 10 March 1946 to further the work.
