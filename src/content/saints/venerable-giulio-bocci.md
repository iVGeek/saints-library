Giulio joined the Order of Friars Minor Capuchin at the convent at Cingoli, Italy at the age of 13; he made his solemn profession on 2 November 1907. Ordained a priest on 21 December 1907. Father Giuseppe was especially devoted to spritual direction, encouraging religious vocations, and to the confessional. He served as novice master at the convent in Camerino, Italy, as a teacher and high school spiritual director, and as a superior in multiple houses. To support those called to the seminary and religious life, he founded the group Work of Vocations in 1932; in 1943, this group became part of the religious congregation he founded with the same mission, the Franciscan Sisters of Vocations (now the Secular Institute of the Franciscan Volunteers of Vocations).
