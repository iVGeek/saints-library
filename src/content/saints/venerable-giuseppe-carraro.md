Ordained on 31 March 1923 in the diocese of Treviso, Italy. Auxiliary bishop of Treviso and titular bishop of Usula on 29 September 1952. Bishop of Vittorio Veneto, Italy on 9 April 1956. Bishop of Verona, Italy from 15 December 1958 until his retirement on 18 May 1978. Served as a Council Father in four sessions of Vatican II.
