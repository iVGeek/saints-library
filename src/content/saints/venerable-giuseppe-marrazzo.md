Born to a pious peasant family. Educated by the Rogationists of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, he joined them on 29 September 1934; he made his profession on 24 December 1940. Ordained a priest on 9 May 1943.
His first mission as a Rogationist priest was to spread devotion to Saint Anthony of Padua. Transferred in 1948 to the Saint Anthony sanctuary in Messina, Sicily where he tried to model his life on Saint Leopold Mandic, and devoted himself to hearing Confession. Assigned to the Rogationist house in Padua, Italy in 1956 to serve as a spiritual director and to encourage new vocations. In 1972 he was transferred to the Rogationist novitiate house in Zagarolo, Italy where he served as vice-superior, and developed ministries to the sick, the elderly, and youth. In 1974 he returned to the sanctuary of Saint Anthony and spent his remaining 18 years devoted to hearing Confessions. He served in the Rogationists for over 50 years, as a priest for nearly 50.
