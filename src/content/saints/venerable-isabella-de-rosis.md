The eldest of nine children born to the 19th century Italian noble family of Baron Domiziano de Roses and Baroness Gabriella Francesca Berlingieri, Isabella was educated at the boarding school of the Santa Chiara monastery in Naples, Italy. She grew up loving the religious life of the sisters who ran the school, and was drawn to the religious life herself; her family objected, wishing her to marry and be part of the secular world.
At age 15, inspired by Saint Margaret Mary Alacoque, she consecrated herself to the Sacred Heart. From 1860 to 1874, ages 18 to 32, she investigated entering several religious orders including the Pia Unione of the Handmaids Oblates of the Sacred Heart, and the Daughters of Charity in Paris, France, but none of them worked out. After devoting herself to prayer, and under the spiritual direction of her parish priest, she started what became the Reparatrix Sisters of the Sacred Heart in Rossano Calabro; the Sisters were dedicated to reparation for offenses against the Sacred Heart. There were initially only two Sisters – and one of them left; but on 24 October 1875 they received approval of her bishop, and the Sisters were officially founded as the Poor Sisters of Reparation of the Most Sacred Heart of Jesus; the Sisters continue their good work in various places today.
