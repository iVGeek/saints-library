István was the middle of five children born to the pious family of Lajos Kaszap and Róza Vinkler. In his youth he was an excellent student, a champion gymnast, and an active member of the Boy Scouts. István was drawn to religious life, and entered the Jesuit novitiate on 31 July 1934 in the seminary at Manréza, Buda, Hungary. Soon his health began to fail, his severe pleurisy was misdiagnosed, and while István received a some treatment, he was forced to withdraw from seminary and died soon after; during the entire painful process he offered all of it to God, and submitted completely to His will.
