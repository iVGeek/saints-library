One of seventeen children born to José Joaquín and Ana Joaquina, Jesús Antonio was baptized at the age of one day; four of the children in his family entered religious life. Jesús Antonio studied at the seminary in Medellín, Colombia, and was ordained a priest on 11 March 1922; he followed his vocation for 49 years. He served as confessor to several religious houses, spiritual director of the Medellín seminary, chaplain for the local school for the blind and deaf, and taught dogmatic theology. He founded the magazine “Sé Apóstol” in 1948 as part of the Eucharistic Crusade in the archdiocese. Founded the Fondo sacerdotal para los pobres (Priestly Fund for the Poor) in 1971 to facilitate priests helping the poor out of their own pockets as he did. Father Jesús Antonio was noted for his piety by all who worked with him, for giving away any personal property he acquired, and for his devotion to the Eucharist, the Sacred Heart, the Virgin Mary, Saint Joseph and Saint Gabriel de la Dolorosa.
