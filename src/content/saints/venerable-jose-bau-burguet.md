After studying at the Colegio Mayor-Seminary of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary in the Temple and Saint Thomas of Villanueva in Valencia, Spain, José was ordained a priest on 19 December 1891. Vicar of the Church of the Purísima Concepción. Vicar of the Church of Santa Monica de Valencia. Parish priest of the Church of the Assumption and Santa Bárbara de Massarrojos. Rector of the Colegio Mayor-Seminary of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary in the Temple and Santo Tomás de Villanueva. Chaplain of the Agustinas Recoletas de Benicalap. Treasurer of the Church of San Miguel and San Sebastián in Valencia. Director of the Religious Doctrine Operators of Alacuás. First president of the Apostolic Union of the Clergy of Valencia.
