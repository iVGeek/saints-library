Born to a deeply religious family, and received a good education, studying in Barcelona and Zaragoza, Spain. Teacher. Priest of the archdiocese of Zaragoza, Spain. He continued teaching for a while, but became so involved in ministry to the poor and sick that he had to give up teaching. Founded the Congregation of the Sisters of Charity of Saint Anne, dedicated to caring for the sick and helpless.
