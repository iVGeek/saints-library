Juana's father worked on the estate of Mother Maria Jesús Herruzo, Foundress of the Congregation of the Worker Sisters of the Heart of Jesus, and when the girl's mother died in 1945, she lived with, was educated by, and two blood sisters joined the Sisters. On 30 October 1950, Juana contracted typhus; she nearly died, was paralyzed, and eventually only recovered the ability to move her head and her hands. With these limitations, Juana had to get special permission to join the Sisters, but she received it and joined the Congregation on 9 March 1966, making her perpetual profession on 19 March 1973. She was bed-ridden, but managed to help the Sisters by sewing and spending much of her time in prayer.
