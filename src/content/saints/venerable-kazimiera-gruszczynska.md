Drawn to religious life as a child, Kazimiera made private vow of chastity at age 10. She renewed this at age 21, and lived as a celebate single woman at her family home. When her mother died in August 1870, Kazimiera took over care of her family home. Outside, she began visiting the sick, and teaching catechism to local children. A spiritual student of Blessed Honorat Kozminski, Kazimiera joined the Messenger Sisters of the Heart of Jesus in 1875, making her profession on 1 June 1879; she served by supervising a boarding school for children, and a home for disabled teachers. With Father Honorat's help, she founded the Franciscan Sisters of Suffering in 1882; the Sisters has a mission to care for the sick and suffering, and Kazimiera served as their director for her remaining 45 years.
