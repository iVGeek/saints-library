One of seven children born to a poor family; as a small boy, he helped his father drive oxen, and when the man died, Libério left school to support the family. In 1902 at age 18 he became a bricklayer's assistant. Four years later he entered seminary, and was ordained priest of the diocese of Divinópolis, Brazil on 20 March 1916. He was completely devoted to his vocation, caring for the sick and the poor, and assigned to several parishes as his superiors knew they could depend on his piety and hard work; he served 64 years in the priesthood.
