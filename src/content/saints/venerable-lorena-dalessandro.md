The eldest of three children, Lorena developed bone cancer as a small child, had a transplant at age 10, and an amputation at age 12. She served as a cantor at her high school and parish Masses, catechist at her parish, was a member of Renewal in the Holy Spirit, and carried all of the work and illness with great courage, faith and encouragement to others.
