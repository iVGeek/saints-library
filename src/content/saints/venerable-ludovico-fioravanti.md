Franciscan Friar Minor, taking the name Gregorio. Priest. Served as a teacher in Rome and Venice, Italy. Elected Provincial Minister of the Lombard Province–Veneta in 1856. Co-founded the Franciscan Missionaries of the Sacred Heart in Gemona del Friuli, Italy in 1859.
