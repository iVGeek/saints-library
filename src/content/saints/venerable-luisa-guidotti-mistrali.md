Born to the Italian nobility, the eldest of three children born to Camillo Guidotti and Anna Mistrali; her mother died when Luisa was in her teens. Member of Catholic Action. Physician with a speciality in radiology. Consecrated member of the Women's Medical Missionary Association. She had a strong devotion to Saint Thomas More. Beginning on 10 August 1966, she served the rest of her life in rural clinics in Rhodesia (modern Zimbabwe), including during the period of the civil war. She died when shot at a roadblock near the All Souls Mission clinic where she worked; she was transporting a pregnant woman to the Mission, and the forces at the roadblock assumed that because she was driving after curfew, she was a hostile.
