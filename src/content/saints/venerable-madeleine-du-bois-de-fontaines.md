Discalced Carmelite nun for over 30 years. Novice mistress for her house. In 1608 she was chosen the first prioress of the Discalced Carmelite sisters in France, serving at the Carmel of the Incarnation in Paris, France. Founded the Carmel of Lyon, France. Founded the Carmel of the Mother of God in Paris in 1617. She wrote about her mystical experiences, and these writings influenced the development of French Carmelite spirituality.
