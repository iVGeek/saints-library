The son of Juan García Blázquez and María Antonia Nieto Sánchez. He studied at the Conciliar Seminary of Salamanca, Spain, and was ordained a priest on 16 May 1920. Parish priest in Cantalapiedra and Santa María de Sando. He joined the Jesuits on 30 July 1926. Served as spiritual director of seminarians at the Comillas Pontifical University.
