At age 19, Marco graduated from the University of Padua with degrees in civil and canon law, and the served as a lawyer for the Apostolic Nunciature of the Republic of Venice. In his 20's he abandoned his career to become a member of the Order of the Regular Clerics of Saint Paul (Barnabites). Ordained a priest on 20 December 1550. In 1558 he joined the Order of Friars Minor in Udine, Italy, taking the name Antonio. In the Order he taught canon law, preached, compiled works on Franciscan spirituality, and wrote on asceticism and mysticism. Participated in the Council of Trent as a Franciscan theologian. Founded several Franciscan houses and Franciscan tertiary congregations in the diocese of Vicenza. Founded the Company of the Dimesse Sisters, Daughters of Mary Immaculate. Worked with plague victims in Venice, Italy in 1576 and 1577. In 1583, he became a hermit in the Berici hills, but in 1586 was ordered back to community life as his example was influencing too many other brothers to become solitaries.
