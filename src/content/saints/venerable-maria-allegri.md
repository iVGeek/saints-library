Member of the Sisters Established in the Charity of the Good Shepherd. She was devoted to the contemplative life, the Eucharist, Mary, and a life of penance. A mystic, she had the gift of prophecy, received visions and the stigmata, and was a counsellor and spiritual teacher to many.
