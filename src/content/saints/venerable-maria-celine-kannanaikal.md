Raised in a pious family, Maria had a great devotion to the Virgin Mary, to Saint Teresa of the Child Jesus, to Saint Maria Goretti, and to time spent in Eucharistic adoration. After finishing her education, she spent three years teaching before following a call to religious life; she joined the Ursuline Sisters of Mary Immaculate in 1954. Her novitiate was difficult as Maria had a mystical nature, given to visions, ecstacies and diabolic harassment; she made her first profession on 20 June 1957. Though she continued to teach, her health rapidly failed and Maria died 5 weeks before she was able to make her solemn profession.
