Diagnosed with a brain tumor at age 4, Maria lived her whole life with the limitations this imposed on her, being open about the problem, never looking for sympathy because of it, and never letting it stop her from her work. She was a Franciscan tertiary, was active in parish life, and helped organize pilgrimages to Lourdes, France. She solicited for charities, had a ministry to the sick, built homes for the elderly, disabled and neglected children, and constructed a small hospital on a river boat that then took medical help to the people along the Amazon River.
