Maria grew up in a pious home, served as a catechist in her parish, and was known for her singing and guitar playing. Answering a call to religious life, she joined the Congregation of the Helping Sisters of Our Lady of Pietà in her late 20's, making her first vows on 19 March 1936, her perpetual profession on 6 January 1941. She was appointed superior of the Sisters' house in Itaúna, Brazil where she set an example of a life of prayer, teaching catechism, and caring for the sick.
