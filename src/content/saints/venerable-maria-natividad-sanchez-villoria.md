Poor Clare nun, joining the Order on 13 May 1923 and making her solemn profession on 1 January 1928. Venerable María spent 68 years in religious life serving as portress, sacristan, novice mistress and abbess. She slept little, and spent her nights in contemplative prayer and Eucharistic adoration. Her sisters considered her to be full of the love of God which she showed by ministering to and sharing her gift of discernment with anyone who came to the convent.
