Raised in a farm family and educated primarily at home by her mother who insured she was trained in domestic and needle work. Maria was always drawn to religious life, and when she was old enough to live on her own, she and some like-minded women began living as nuns. On 8 December 1948, with the approval of Archbishop Arcangelo Mazzotti of Sassari, Italy, she founded the Daughters of Mater Purissima (Daughters of the Most Pure Mother).
