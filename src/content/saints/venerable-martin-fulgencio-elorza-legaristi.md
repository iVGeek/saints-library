Member of the Passionists, joining in 1912 and making his solemn profession on 12 June 1924. Ordained a priest on 13 July 1924. He served as director of students in Gabiria, Spain, then Provincial Councilor, and then Provincial Superior, serving during the persecutions of the Spanish Civil War.
In 1947, Father Martin was chosen delegate for the Passionists in America. First bishop of the territorial prelature of Moyobamba, Peru on 15 January 1949. He travelled his territory on horseback or canoe, recruited priests and religious to missionary work, supported the poor, and built churches in an area often hostile to Christians. Chosen titular bishop of Baliana on 3 October 1953. Served in sessions one, two and four of Vatican II.
