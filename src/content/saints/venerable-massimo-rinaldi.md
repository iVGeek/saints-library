Ordained a priest on 6 July 1893, serving in the Italian cities of Rieti, Ornaro, Greccio, and Montefiascone. He joined the Scalabrinians in 1900, making his profession on 4 November 1900, and served as missionay in Brazil from then until 1910. He returned to Italy for the General Chapter of the order, and served in administrative positions including treasurer. Chosen bishop of Rieti, Italy by Pope Pius XI on 2 August 1924; he was installed on 19 March 1925. Bishop Massimo worked to reform religious life the houses in his diocese, and to improve social conditions for the laity.
