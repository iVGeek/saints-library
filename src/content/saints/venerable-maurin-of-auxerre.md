Bishop of Auxerre, France from 772 until his death, serving for 28 years. Described in documents of the time as a “vigorous, eloquent, cultured and holy man”. He cared for the poor, and got Blessed Charlemagne to help recover property and goods previously taken from the diocese. Though some old manuscripts list him as Saint, the diocese of Auxerre gives him the title Venerable.
