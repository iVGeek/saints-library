Initially a law student, Miquel began studying at the University of Barcelona in 1872, but abandoned the field, and lived for a while in Paris, France. Finally answering a call to the priesthood, he began studying at Gregorian University in Rome, Italy on 24 July 1883. Received in audience by Pope Leo XIII in June 1886. He earned a degree in theology and was ordained a priest in 1888. Served as canon of the cathedral chapter of the diocese of Mallorca, Spain. Poet in the Castilian language.
