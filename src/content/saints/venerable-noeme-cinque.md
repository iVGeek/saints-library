Second of twelve children born to Vincent and Sarah Cinque. Though she felt drawn to religious life from an early age, she worked as a teacher and a nurse; she would often visit the sick and elderly at home. Catechist. Member of Catholic Action, the Daughters of Mary, and the Apostleship of Prayer. Nun in the Congregation of the Adorers of the Blood of Christ. She was assigned to an area of the Amazon with no physicans, and became the de facto medical team for an entire region, teaching nutrition and hygiene as well as caring for the sick. In 1971 she was assigned to Altamira, Brazil, an area with an exploding population due to the construction of the Trans-Amazonian Highway; she helped found the Divine Providence Home for pregnant women and infants, especially caring for those who were homeless.
