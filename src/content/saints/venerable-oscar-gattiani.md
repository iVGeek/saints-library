Oscar grew up helping in the small grocery his parents operated. In 1929, at age 15, he began the Capuchin novitiate at the convent in Cesena, Italy, and was given the name Fra Guglielmo; he made his solemn profession in Bologna, Italy on 8 December 1935. He was ordained a priest on 23 May 1938. He taught in several Franciscan seminaries, and then in 1946 became novice master in Cesena; he served for 18 years. In 1964 he was chosen spiritual director of novices, and served 11 years. In those decades he became known in the city for his spirituality and wisdom, and his ministry to the sick. Pilgrim to the Holy Land for six months in 1980. In October 1980 he was assigned to be porter and confessor at the Sanctuary of the Holy Crucifix in Faenza, where he served for another 19 years; from 6 in the morning until midnight each day he would listen to to the sufferings of visitors, give them wise counsel, and at night offer them each and all to God.
