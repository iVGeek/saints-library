Studied at the Escuelas Pías de Tolosa in Guipúzcoa, Spain, a school run by the Piarists. He joined the Piarists himself in Zaragoza, Spain, making his solemn profession on 25 March 1935. Later in 1935 he began teaching kindergarten in Zaragoza, a position where he would work for the next 45 years. Ordained a priest on 18 April 1937, he added ministry to the poor and sick to his work in education. He co-wrote a series of books on teaching languages. Father Pedro was award a silver Medal of Merit by the civil authorities in 1973 for his work in education, and a square next to the school where he worked is dedicated to him.
