Baptized at the age of one day. As a young man in Rome, Italy, he earned degrees in civil and canon law, and studied archaeology. Ordained a priest on 10 June 1702. Appointed Titular Archbishop of Athenae on 7 November 1707. Elevated to Cardinal in Pectore on 18 May 1712. Cardinal–Priest of San Giovanni a Porta Latina on 21 November 1712. Founder of the Suore Collegine della Sacra Famiglia (Congregation of the Collegine Sisters of the Holy Family) with a mission to teach religion to girls and young women; the Sister continue their good work today in Albania, the United Kingdom, Mexico, Romania, Tanzania and in Kenya. Prefect of the Congregation of the Council in 1718. Camerlengo of the Apostolic Chamber from 1719 to 1720. Participated in the conclave of 1721 that elected Pope Innocent XIII. Pro-Datary of the Apostolic Dataria from 1721 to 1730. Participated in the conclave of 1724 that elected Pope Benedict XIII. Cardinal–Priest of Santa Maria in Trastevere on 11 September 1726. Participated in the conclave of 1730 that elected Pope Clement XII. Cardinal–Bishop of Frascati on 15 December 1734. Participated in the conclave of 1740 that elected Pope Benedict XIV.
