Born to a peasant family. Attracted to Franciscan spirituality, in 1888, at age 18, Rocco joined the Order of Friars Minor, taking the name Fra Giuseppe; he made his solemn profession on 13 March 1892. Ordained a priest in Palestrina, Italy on 22 September 1894. He served in the convents of San Francesco a Ripa in Rome, Italy, and Fonte Colombo near Rieta, Italy, often serving as novice master of the houses where he instilled a deep Franciscan spirituality in the novices. During World War II he ministered to all, and helped hide and save those persecuted by the Fascist authorities.
