Born to a farm family, the third son of Miquel Gili i Literas and Antònia Vives i Artigues. Ordained a diocesan priest on 4 April 1835, he was known for a life dedicated to the sick and needy. He became an Augustinian tertiary in 1858, became work on organizing a religious congregation, and founded the Congregation of the Augustinian Daughters of Empar on 6 February 1859. Father Sebastià served as superior of the Congregation, and founded a school affiliated with it. He worked to re-structure the charitable institutions of Mallorca to help them operate more efficiently, and provide more of their resouces to the poor. Appointed canon of the cathedral in Palma on 30 July 1883. In June 1894 he retired from public and pastoral life, spending his remaining few months at the Congregation mother-house.
