Baptized at the age of two days, Vicenta early felt a call to religious life. She started her notiviate on 25 October 1901 in Salmanca, Spain, and made her solemn profession in the Daughters of Jesus on 8 December 1909. She and five sister Daughters left Spain on 30 September 1911 for missionary work in Brazil. Led by Father Bruno Alberdi, the group travelled by train, then horseback, and then on foot, arriving at the town of Pirenópolis, Brazil on 7 November 1911. There the sisters set up a school for girls, and Vicenta devoted herself to it for the next 16 years. In 1927 she was transferred to Leopoldina, Brazil where she served as porter for 33 years.
